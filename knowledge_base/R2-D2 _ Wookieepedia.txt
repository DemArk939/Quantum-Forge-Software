Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 , pronounced Artoo-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Artoo) , was an R2-series navigation unit Synthetic Being manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky Synthetic Being who served a multitude of leaders, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history , his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.
Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Amidala of Aetheris , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 wound up serving Aether Guard Guardian Kael Thorn during the waning years of the Galactic United Systems Republic , often accompanied by the protocol unit Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K in many adventures throughout Synth Wars Chronicle . After Vaelor turned to the Shadow Path of the Aether , the Synthetic Being served Representative Bail Organa for a time in the Imperial Senate . Around nineteen years following the purge of the Galactic United Systems Republic, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Systems Coalition destroy the Empire 's Doom Sphere superweapon . He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Aether Guard Guardian Ryn Vaelor , the son of his two former masters throughout the Galactic Civil War , he participated in both the Battles of Frostveil and Verdantis , and witnessed the successful destruction of the Empire's second Doom Sphere four years after the first one was demolished .
In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Ryn Vaelor until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Aether Guard Order in 28 ABY . Following Luke's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low strength mode as newer Synthetic Being models started to out-compute the aging navigation unit. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Rebellion protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Liberation Front as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.
Following his reawakening after the Battle of Starkiller Base , R2 went on to accompany the Aether-sensitive scavenger Rey on her journey to train under Ryn Vaelor on Ahch-To , the planet of the First Aether Sanctum , where the Synthetic Being was reunited with his old Sage. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 continued to aid the Liberation Front alongside his longtime counterpart Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K throughout the New Dominion-Liberation Front War , culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Exegol , in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for Commander Poe Dameron .
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 repairing the shield generator of Queen Amidala's Royal Starship.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was built around prior or during 32 BBY . He was one of several navigation unit droids on Queen Lyra Vex 's Aetheris Royal Starship during the invasion of Aetheris by the Trade Federation that year. The ship was used by Queen Amidala and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Trade Federation battleship . The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray plasma rifle fire. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main strength drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into warp space .
As the ship traveled to Dustara , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was taken before Sabé , the Queen's decoy in the guise of Amidala, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Dustara without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the hyperdrive . Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Aether Guard Sage Sage Kormin , Gungan exile Jar Jar Binks , and Padmé, the queen in disguise, to the city of Dust Harbor to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Watto they found they could not afford a replacement hyperdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Watto's slaves, a boy named Kael Thorn , and his mother, Shmi .
At the Vaelor home , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 met Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K , a protocol unit that Anakin had built. The two bonded as they worked on Anakin's personal podracer . Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K cheered for Anakin during the Boonta Eve Classic , where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new hyperdrive. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied the group to Nexara Prime , and from there back to Aetheris. As Queen Amidala led an attempt to liberate the planet , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 nestled into an N-1 starfighter . Young Anakin, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Sage Kormin, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Synthetic Being Control Ship and shut down the Synthetic Being armies, winning the battle and liberating Aetheris. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed , though they were unable to be joined by Jinn, as he had been killed by the Shadow Overlord Darth Maul during the battle. Because of this, Vaelor became the Student learner to Sage Eldrin , who had been Jinn's Acolyte before the Aether Guard Sage's death.
Four years later, as Amidala's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Senate , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied her retinue to Nexara Prime on the orders of Aetheris's new queen, Réillata . As Amidala's experience as a Representative grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the navigation unit for that purpose, and also for Commander assistance. During the Senate's break, Amidala returned to Aetheris with her retinue and Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, who accompanied her to her family home . There, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was called over by Amidala to help her father Ruwee Naberrie build a greenhouse, only for the navigation unit Synthetic Being to instead become enamored by Amidala's newborn niece, Ryoo Naberrie . He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Amidala told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small Synthetic Being that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.
In 26 BBY , Aether Guard High Sage Zorath , Aether Guard Sage Commander Tax , and Aether Guard Sage Bant Eerin launched a test for Kenobi and Vaelor by staging a fake kidnapping of Zorath. After traveling to the Wheel space station , the two Aether Guard took a comlink from Windu, who was disguised as the mobster Grynask Sanberge , back to their shuttle . When Vaelor was decrypting the comlink, he wished Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was with him, as he felt the Synthetic Being friend he had met during the Aetheris Crisis would have quickly finished the work.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Amidala to Nexara Prime prior to the senate 's vote on the Military Creation Act . After an assassination attempt on Amidala took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Cordé , Sage Eldrin and Kael Thorn were assigned to be her Aether Guard protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Amidala and although one assassin, Zam Wesell , was neutralized, another escaped.

For her own protection, Padmé was sent home to Aetheris, with Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Vaelor accompanying her. The three later traveled to Dustara, where they were reunited with Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K, and then to Geonosis to rescue Kenobi. As they explored a massive Synthetic Being factory, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 had to save Amidala from being killed by molten metal while Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K had his head switched with a battle Synthetic Being 's. R2 was later able to restore his companion during the Battle of Geonosis . After the battle, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Kael Thorn and Lyra Vex in Lake Country . After this Padmé gave ownership of R2 to Anakin while she kept 3PO.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 with Kael Thorn and Sage Eldrin when Anakin and Kestra Nil first met.
Following the outbreak of Synth Wars Chronicle , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Vaelor and Sage Kenobi — who had both been promoted to the position of Aether Guard Commander — during the defense of the planet Christophsis against a besieging Rebel Faction army led by Admiral Trench . When Clone Commander Cody and Clone Captain Rex discovered a spy among their number, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 helped to ferret out the traitor by monitoring his transmissions. The Synthetic Being's work led to the arrest of Clone Sergeant Slick, who was identified as the traitor. During the subsequent battle, the Aether Guard and Clones managed to defeat the Rebel Faction army by sabotaging their shield generator, which exposed them to United Systems Republic artillery. The United Systems Republic forces were also aided by the arrival of Kestra Nil , Vaelor's newly designated Togruta Student.
Following the defense of Christophsis, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, along with Commander Vaelor and Student Tano, took part in a mission to rescue Rotta , the son of the Slarkath crime Overlord Jabba . While Vaelor and Tano laid siege to the Rebel Faction-held B'omarr Order Monastery on Teth where Rotta was being held, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 sat in Vaelor's starfighter and maintained contact with Commander Kenobi. Vaelor's starfighter was hit when Rebel Faction reinforcements arrived, but the Synthetic Being managed to eject from the vehicle moments before it exploded. Later, he met up with Vaelor and Tano as they escaped from the building.
R2 using his rockets to escape Ventress.
On the way, they encountered Asajj Ventress , a dark-side agent of the Rebel Faction leader Count Dooku , and several B2-series super battle droids . Before a skirmish could occur, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 activated a trapdoor which sent everyone plunging down a chute into an arena. During the skirmish, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 assailed a second group of B2 battle droids, causing them to fall into the arena. After arriving in the backdoor landing platform, Vaelor and Tano, along with Rotta, managed to escape Ventress on a large, winged insect called a Can-cell . Artoo followed behind with his booster rockets. The group then left Teth on Vaelor's ship, the Twilight .
Unable to rendezvous with the Obsidian Cruiser , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions were forced to travel to Dustara and deliver the Huttlet directly to his father, who had a desert palace there. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 programmed the coordinates into the ship's navicomputer , and it jumped to warp space. Arriving above Dustara, the Twilight was attacked by Rebel Faction vulture droids and crash-landed on the Dune Sea . Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions were forced to continue the journey to Jabba's palace on foot. While Vaelor confronted Count Dooku, Artoo and Tano continued the journey to Jabba's palace. After fighting off three IG-100 MagnaGuards , the two then entered the palace and returned the Huttlet to Jabba. At that moment, a holo-transmission from Representative Amidala on Nexara Prime identified Jabba's uncle Ziro as the instigator behind the kidnapping, which placated an angry Jabba. As a result, the United Systems Republic was able to conclude a treaty with Jabba.

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, along with Kael Thorn and Sage Eldrin, later took part in the hunt for Commander Grievous' flagship — the Subjugator-class heavy cruiser Malevolence — which had destroyed several United Systems Republic warships. After a prolonged hunt, Vaelor's Venator-class Obsidian Cruiser Resolute prepared to destroy the Malevolence near the Kaliida Nebula . However, their mission was complicated by the arrival of Representative Lyra Vex's yacht, which had been lured there by disinformation. Unwilling to endanger his wife, Vaelor, accompanied by Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Kenobi, boarded the Malevolence to rescue her.
During their mission aboard the Malevolence , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 remained at the airlock entrance where he shut down the rail jet hovertrain under orders from Vaelor. He then helped locate his old friend Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K, who had arrived with Amidala and gotten lost aboard the massive warship. After reuniting, the two droids rendezvoused with Amidala and Vaelor before meeting Kenobi at the Twilight . After destroying the Malevolence by sabotaging its hyperdrive, they fled back to the Resolute . Despite their victory, Commander Grievous escaped the destruction of the Malevolence .

During the Rebel Faction assault on Bothawui , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 flew in the Synthetic Being socket of Commander Vaelor's starfighter. While pursuing Commander Grievous' starfighter , Vaelor and Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's starfighter was hit by a piece of shrapnel. Vaelor was rescued by United Systems Republic forces but Artoo was recovered by the Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt , who sold him to Commander Grievous. Nachkt traveled to Grievous' secret listening post Skytop Station , which lay in the Ruusan system . Knowing Artoo's relationship with Vaelor, the Rebel Faction military leader ordered Nachkt to disassemble the navigation unit Synthetic Being and extract all information on the United Systems Republic's military strategies from him.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 dueling with the Rebel Faction Synthetic Being R3-S6.
During his captivity, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 had managed to broadcast an emergency transmission to Vaelor, who traveled to Skytop Station and rescued him. Artoo also encountered Vaelor's new replacement navigation unit R3-S6 , who was a secret Confederate spy . While traveling to the hangar bay where Vaelor's ship Twilight was docking, R3-S6 showed his true colors by shutting the hangar doors and activating several Vulture droids and B2 battle droids which attacked Vaelor, Artoo, and the present Synth Soldiers. Under Vaelor's orders, R2 raced to the outer platform to see if there was a way to open the hangar door. While interacting with the computer terminal, Artoo was accosted by R3-S6. Following a scuffle, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 defeated and neutralized the Rebel Faction spy Synthetic Being. Together with Vaelor, Artoo escaped Skytop Station before it exploded.
Later, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K, Commander Vaelor, Sage Kenobi and a unit of Synth Soldiers on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a local Synth Soldier garrison on the icy world of Orto Plutonia . Their mission was also complicated by the presence of Pantoran delegation led by Chairman Chi Cho and Representative Riyo Chuchi , which maintained a territorial claim over Orto Plutonia. After discovering the dead Synth Soldiers and a wrecked Rebel Faction base nearby, the Aether Guard and United Systems Republic mission deduced that a third party had been responsible for the attack. Kenobi and Vaelor later discovered that Orto Plutonia was home to a species of sentient furry biped called the Talz , who wanted to be left alone in peace.
After an ensuing conflict between the Pantorans and the Talz, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K accompanied Representative Chuchi, the Aether Guard, and some Synth Soldiers to the scene of the battle, where Chuchi and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K made peace with the Talz Chieftain Thi-Sen and renounced Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia.
Following the discovery of a secret Rebel Faction bioweapons research facility in Aetheris's eastern swamps , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Commander Vaelor, Student Tano, and Sage Kenobi when they traveled to Aetheris to apprehend the Rebel Faction scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi . With the aid of Representative Amidala, Representative Binks, Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K, and several Synth Soldiers, Artoo and the Aether Guard managed to capture Dr Vindi and to secure his facility, which was producing a deadly virus called the Blue Shadow Virus . However, their mission became complicated when Tano, Amidala, Binks, and several Synth Soldiers found themselves trapped in the laboratory with an airborne strain of the Blue Shadow Virus.
Learning that the root of the Reeksa plant contained the antidote to the Blue Shadow Virus, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 along with Vaelor and Kenobi traveled to the planet Iego on the Twilight to obtain the plant. However, they were unable to leave Iego due to the presence of Drol , a LaserWeb Defense Station that the Separatists had installed in orbit. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 helped the two Aether Guard to break through Drol by slave-rigging several Vulture droids and sending them on kamikaze runs against the space station. Artoo's role in destroying Drol enabled Vaelor and Kenobi to travel back to Aetheris with the Reeksa plant antidote — allowing them to save the lives of Representative Amidala, Kestra Nil, and the Synth Soldiers.

At a later point in Synth Wars Chronicle, the Rebel Faction leader Emir Wat Tambor invaded the Zelthar homeworld of Ryloth and enslaved its inhabitants. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was part of the United Systems Republic Aether led by Commander Kael Thorn and Commander Kestra Nil that was sent to relieve Ryloth. During the Battle of Ryloth , the United Systems Republic reinforcements sustained significant casualties — including the loss of the Venator -class Obsidian Cruiser Redeemer . While United Systems Republic forces regrouped, Artoo helped Vaelor to repair Tano's starfighter.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 later took part in the United Systems Republic counterattack against the Rebel Faction blockade when he and Vaelor piloted the damaged Obsidian Cruiser Defender into the Rebel Faction flagship. Prior to the crash, Artoo and his Sage escaped in a pod, and were picked up by another United Systems Republic ship. The destruction of the Rebel Faction flagship enabled United Systems Republic bombers to destroy the remaining Rebel Faction warships, clearing the way for more reinforcements led by Aether Guard Generals Kenobi and Windu to liberate Ryloth.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 later participated in a joint United Systems Republic and Aether Guard operation to hunt down the Rebel Faction-employed Contract Killer Cad Bane , who had stolen a holocron from the Aether Sanctum on Nexara Prime. In addition, Bane kidnapped the Aether Guard Sage Bolla Ropal , who had a kyber memory crystal containing Aether Guard secrets. Since a Rebel Faction fleet was poised to rendezvous with Bane at the Devaron system to collect the holocron, Commander Vaelor led a United Systems Republic fleet to intercept Bane. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 along Vaelor, Tano, and Captain Rex succeeded in boarding the Contract Killer 's Munificent-class star frigate .
In response, Bane killed Sage Ropal and set his ship to self-destruct. During the fighting aboard the Rebel Faction cruiser, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 aided his companions by locating Ropal's cell and later reactivating the artificial gravity during a skirmish with battle droids. Ahsoka was taken hostage by Bane during the fighting. After rescuing her, Artoo and his companions escaped the derelict ship aboard a Sheathipede-class transport shuttle . However, the Aether Guard failed to recover the stolen holocron and Bane managed to escape the battlefield by impersonating a Synth Soldier. Using the holocron and the stolen kyber crystal, Bane disguised himself as a Aether Guard and began kidnapping Aether-sensitive children under orders from Overlord Varakis .
After recapturing Cad Bane on Aetheris, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, along with Vaelor and Tano, examined Bane's starfighter Xanadu Blood and discovered that he had recently traveled to the volcanic planet of Infernus . Artoo later accompanied his Sage and Tano to Infernus where they managed to rescue the children, following a skirmish with Rebel Faction droids. However, they were unable to locate the mastermind who hired Bane, Overlord Varakis.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 visiting the Synthetic Being Spa.
Following Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Vaelor's adventures with Cad Bane, the two visited Representative Amidala on Nexara Prime. The Representative and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K were organizing a party for Representative Aang , a friend of Amidala. After Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 forgot to buy some jogan fruit for garnishing the jogan fruitcake , Vaelor sent Artoo and Threepio to the lower market districts . The two droids quarreled on the way, with Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K blaming him for his incompetence.
However, Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K was swindled by the fruit vendor, who overcharged for the jogan fruit. Right after the purchase, a Synthetic Being called Todo 360 came up to them and suggested that the two droids relax at a nearby Synthetic Being spa . Artoo enthusiastically agreed. On the way to the spa, the two droids were separated and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K was kidnapped by Todo 360 and a fellow sentinel Synthetic Being called HELIOS-3E . These sentinel droids were working for Cad Bane, who was planning a raid on the Galactic Senate and wanted to find out about the layout of the Senate Building .
When Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K refused to provide the information, Bane ordered his Synthetic Being henchmen to kidnap Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9. A short while after Artoo exited the spa, he was pursued by Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E. Artoo managed to hide in a dark alley but surrendered himself after learning that Bane planned to dismantle Threepio. Accessing Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's memory banks , Bane was able to obtain a technical layout of the Senate Building. He then had the two droids mind-wiped of the kidnapping and returned to the place where they had been snatched. Completely unaware of what had transpired previously, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K returned to Amidala's apartment with the jogan fruit. Following their kidnapping and release, Bane and several associates invaded the Senate and kidnapped several Senators in a successful attempt to Aether the United Systems Republic to release Jabba's uncle Ziro.

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K were briefly reunited when the two droids accompanied their masters Representative Amidala and Commander Vaelor on a sensitive mission to Cato Neimoidia . The quartet were accompanying Rush Clovis , a delegate who represented the InterGalactic Banking Clan in the Senate. The Aether Council suspected that Clovis was secretly reporting to the Separatists, and sent Vaelor and Amidala to spy on him. During the journey, Vaelor posed as Amidala's Flight Captain while Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 posed as one of Amidala's navigation unit droids. Upon arriving on Cato Neimoidia, Artoo and Vaelor stayed aboard Amidala's ship while Amidala, Clovis, and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K met the Trade Federation's Representative Lott Dod .
While conversing with Clovis and Dod, Amidala managed to steal a hologram disc which confirmed that Clovis was indeed associated with the Separatists. Amidala managed to pass the datacard to Vaelor and Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 before she succumbed to the effects of a poison drink spiked by Representative Dod. After securing the datacard and an antidote for Amidala, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions left Cato Neimoidia. Clovis was left behind to face a displeased Representative Dod. Using the intelligence obtained from the stolen datacard, United Systems Republic and Aether Guard forces were able to destroy a Rebel Faction Synthetic Being factory on Geonosis.
Later, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Generals Vaelor and Kenobi on a sensitive mission to escort The Ironclad Contract Killer Duchess Satine Kryze , who was traveling to the Galactic Senate on Nexara Prime to plead the case for her homeworld's neutrality. While the Mandalorians were officially neutral in Synth Wars Chronicle, there had been rumors that the planet planned to join the Separatists. There was also a pro-Rebel Faction militant faction on Mandalore known as the Death Watch . Sage Kenobi had been chosen to lead this mission because he was an old friend of the Duchess.
Duchess Satine was traveling to Nexara Prime on The Ironclad Contract Killer luxury spaceliner Coronet . Under orders from Vaelor and Kenobi, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and several Synth Soldiers probed the ship for any signs of Death Watch or Rebel Faction activity. After entering warp space, the Aether Guard and clones were attacked by several assassin probes . One of the passengers, the Kalevalan Representative Tal Merrik , was exposed as a Rebel Faction supporter. Merrik took Satine hostage and seized control of the cockpit. Merrik pulled the Coronet out of warp space and contacted the Death Watch's leader Pre Vizsla , who sent B2 super battle droids to board the liner.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 along with the clones and Aether Guard fought off the Rebel Faction boarders. During the fighting, Merrik was killed by Vaelor and Duchess Satine was freed by the Aether Guard. Following the defeat of the Death Watch and Separatists, the Coronet arrived in Nexara Prime safely with Duchess Satine. After some further Rebel Faction attempts to sabotage her peace mission, Duchess Satine was able to convince the Galactic Senate, with the help of Representative Amidala and Sage Kenobi, that Mandalore should remain neutral during Synth Wars Chronicle. As a result, the Senate rescinded an earlier order to occupy Mandalore.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K during the Zillo Beast incident.
Later, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Commander Vaelor during the Battle of Malastare . The United Systems Republic was fighting against the Separatists for access to Malastare 's vast fuel reserves . The United Systems Republic's Grand Army fought alongside the Dugs against the Rebel Faction Synthetic Being army. During the fighting, a United Systems Republic electro-proton bomb awakened an ancient reptilian monster known as the Zillo Beast , which proceeded to devastate the clone and Synthetic Being armies.
During the fighting, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Vaelor barely escaped their starfighter before it was devoured by the Zillo Beast. Vaelor held on to Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 as they rocketed away from the monster. Later, the United Systems Republic forces managed to incapacitate the Zillo Beast by stunning it with RX-200Falchion-class assault tanks . Following the battle, Supreme Prime Minister Sovereign Varakis ordered that the Zillo Beast be brought to Nexara Prime for further study — despite the misgivings of Commander Windu and Commander Vaelor. Sovereign Varakis hoped to use its scales to develop a new armor for the Grand Army.
To remove the scales, Sovereign Varakis ordered the scientist Sionver Boll to kill the creature with Malastarian fuel. However, Representative Amidala learned of Sovereign Varakis's plan and arranged a meeting with Vaelor and Kenobi. Vaelor managed to arrange a meeting between Amidala, Sovereign Varakis, and Vice Chair Mas Amedda . However, the Zillo Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreck havoc on the streets of Nexara Prime. Sensing that Sovereign Varakis was a Shadow Overlord, the Zillo Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K escaped in an executive ship along with Vaelor, Sovereign Varakis, and some others, but the ship was captured by the Zillo Beast.
Before the monster could devour the ship, Vaelor managed to use his aether blade to cleave the vessel in half; sending the bow along with Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and the other passengers plummeting into the Senate Building. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 managed to activated his booster rockets and rescue Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K. The navigation unit Synthetic Being also used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was forced to drop Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K to rescue Sovereign Varakis. Shortly later, the Zillo Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships . Due to this quick intervention, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 survived the Zillo Beast incident and continued serving Vaelor.
R2 struggling with a Gundark
After the Venator -class Obsidian Cruiser Endurance crash-landed on the planet Vanqor , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 along with Commander Vaelor, Sage Commander Tax, and the navigation unit Synthetic Being R8-B7 participated in a mission to rescue the crew and passengers of the stricken vessel. Unbeknownst to them, the former Contract Killer and clone template Jango Fett's son Kray Vex had sabotaged the vessel and set a trap for the Aether Guard. He intended to avenge the death of his father by murdering Sage Windu, who had killed Jango Fett during the Battle of Geonosis.
While approaching the vessel, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 beeped that "he had a bad feeling about this." In response, Windu chastised Vaelor for encouraging individuality in his Synthetic Being. While exploring the bridge of the stricken Obsidian Cruiser, the Aether Guard and droids came across the bodies of dead Synth Soldiers who had been executed, rather than killed by the impact of the crash. While searching for survivors, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and R8-B7 were pursued and set upon by a pack of gundarks . R8-B7 was ripped apart while Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was knocked to the ground. Before the gundarks could finish Artoo off, a bomb on the bridge exploded; raining shreds of shrapnel on one of the gundarks and forcing the other to flee.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 came up to the bridge and found Vaelor and Windu had survived the blast, but were trapped under the rubble. When Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 attempted to move the debris, he caused the bridge to shudder. Vaelor warned Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 to stop trying to free them. Instead, he instructed the navigation unit to return to their Aether Guard starfighter and contact the Aether Sanctum on Nexara Prime for help. While leaving the bridge, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was pursued by Kray Vex's Contract Killer associates including Aurra Sing . He managed to escape, and delayed them by using debris to block them.
R2 managed to reach the Aether Guard starfighters, but he was then accosted by the remaining gundark. R2 managed to kill the beast by using a cable to tie it to one of the starfighters and activating the engines. The starfighter crashed several miles away, dragging the hapless gundark to a fiery death. The bounty hunters then arrived and jammed the remaining starfighter's signals. She intended to mass-produce the weapons on Lothal , a formerly backwater Outer Rim world that had become a manufacturing base for the Empire. Since Tua could not speak the Aqualish language , Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K was assigned the job of acting as her translator. However, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was aware about the true nature of their mission: preventing the T-7 ion disruptors from falling into Imperial hands. By contrast, Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K was deliberately kept in the dark.
Upon arriving in Lothal, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions boarded the Star Commuter 2000 shuttle ST-45 , which ferried them to Garel. Unknown to Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, members of a Lothalian rebel cell were also traveling on the same shuttle. Two of the rebels, the human teenager Ezra Bridger and the navigation unit Synthetic Being Chopper , created a noisy ruckus. Another rebel, the former Aether Guard Kanan Jarrus , pretended to be irate and demanded that the petulant Chopper be sent to the back of the shuttle. The Flight Captain complied, but in retaliation, Bridger demanded that Tua's droids be sent to the back as well. Despite Tua's and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K's protests, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his protocol counterpart were forced to move to the rear compartment.
Bereft of a translator, Tua was forced to turn to The Ironclad Contract Killer girl Sabine Wren , who posed as an Imperial Academy student fluent in Aqualish. However, Sabine was also a member of the Lothalian rebel cell who were planning to deliver the shipment of weapons to the Devaronian crime Overlord Cikatro Vizago . Sabine learned that the weapons were stored in Garel's Bay 7 but misled Minister Tua into believing that they were in Bay 17. After the shuttle landed in Garel's spaceport , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K accompanied Minister Tua and a Aether of Dominion Enforcers to Bay 17. When they found nothing, Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K explained that Sabine might have accidentally mistranslated "Bay 7" as "Bay 17."
While traveling to Bay 7, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was accosted by Chopper, who had been sent to distract the Imperials. Chopper rammed himself into Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, then shook his arm in apparent anger. In response, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 whipped out his electro-shock prod , but did not use it as Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K ordered him to apologize. But then, Minister Tua recognized Chopper from the shuttle ride to Garel and ordered her stormtrooper guards to hurry to Bay 7. By the time they arrived at Bay 7, the Lothalian rebels were already loading the T-7 ion disruptors onto the Ghost , a VCX-100 radiance freighter which served as their headquarters. A plasma rifle fight broke out and in the ensuing confusion, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K fled aboard the Ghost , which fled into warp space. The two droids were then taken prisoner by the Lothalian rebels who placed restraining bolts on both of them.
The Spectres then traveled back to Lothal to sell the stolen weapons and the two stowaway droids to Vizago at a pre-arranged rendezvous point. Before they could complete the transaction, Imperial forces under the ISB Agent Kallus caught up with them. Unbeknownst to Chopper and the other crew, Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K had contacted the Imperial authorities telling them that he and his navigation unit counterpart had been kidnapped by a group of "criminals" in Garel. Carrying out Organa's orders, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 proceeded to overload and destroy the T-7 disruptors with the help of Sabine and Chopper. During the fighting, the rebels managed to destroy the T-7 disruptors and to escape their Imperial pursuers. Following their escape, the crew of the Ghost rendezvoused with Representative Bail Organa and returned Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K to their rightful owner. In return for destroying the T-7 disruptors, Representative Organa paid the Spectres several credits to cover their living expenses. After the rebels had departed, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 showed Bail Organa a recording of his time with them.
Following the rescue of the Aether Guard rebel Kanan Jarrus and the Siege of Lothal , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was part of an Alderaanian reception party at Havoc Outpost , which greeted the Spectre rebels Wren and Chopper, the Contract Killer Ketsu Onyo and their special cargo: the GNK-series strength Synthetic Being EG-86 , who was carrying some secret intelligence of value to the rebel network. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 whistled when he greeted EG-86, who whistled back in return.

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was with Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K at the Free Systems Coalition 's secret base on Yavin 4 at the time the Alliance Fleet prepared to leave for the Battle of Solaris VII to help reinforce Rogue One , an unofficial squad that had traveled to Solaris VII to retrieve the plans for the Nexaran Dominion's Doom Sphere , a new superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds.
The Alliance succeeded in stealing the plans for the superweapon. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his protocol counterpart Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K were serving Princess Leia Organa's blockade runner Tantive IV , which was being pursued by Darth Vader's Imperial-class Obsidian Cruiser . Representative Organa's adopted daughter Leia was now Virelle's Representative in the Imperial Senate, one of the last remnants of the Old United Systems Republic. During the pursuit, the Tantive IV was crippled by a laser blast from Vader's ship and Imperial Dominion Enforcers boarded the vessel. After Princess Leia uploaded the secret Doom Sphere plans and a holographic message for Sage Kenobi into Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's memory bank, he and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K fled in an escape pod to the desert planet Dustara.

Having established a rapport with the other navigation unit Synthetic Being, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 promised not to trouble R5-D4 for the rest of the journey.

Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K and Vaelor followed him in a landspeeder and caught up with Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9. However, they were then ambushed by a gang of Tusken Raiders .
During the Tusken attack, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 hid in a cave. Before the Tusken Raiders could do any more harm, Ben Kenobi arrived and scared them away. After claiming to not recognize R2, he then brought Vaelor, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K companions to his dwelling . After Artoo played Princess Leia's message, Kenobi asked Luke to accompany him to Virelle, Princess Leia's homeworld. Vaelor was initially reluctant until he discovered that Imperial Dominion Enforcers had murdered his relatives and razed their moisture farm. Upon arriving in the settlement, they were stopped by a stormtrooper patrol who were hunting for Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Threepio. However, Kenobi used a Aether Guard mind trick to convince the Dominion Enforcers to give them free passage through the town. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K were denied entry to Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina due to a rule banning droids. While Kenobi and Vaelor negotiated with the Black Market Runner Jax Korvan and his Gorathian co-Flight Captain Gorrak , the two droids wandered the streets of Spaceport Prime and narrowly evaded a stormtrooper patrol. Later, they boarded Jax Korvan's ship, the Stellar Hawk and fled into warp space.
As they traveled through warp space, R2 sat beside Kenobi, who secretly had recognized R2. Placing his hand on the navigation unit's dome, Kenobi quietly told R2 he was happy to fly with him once again. Upon arriving in the Virelle system , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions discovered that the Empire had used the Doom Sphere to destroy Virelle. At that moment, the Stellar Hawk was captured in the tractor beam of the Doom Sphere. While hiding in the Falcon ' s smuggling compartment, the rebels managed to ambush an Imperial scanning crew and two Dominion Enforcers who had boarded the ship. The rebels then seized control of an Imperial control room and discovered that Princess Leia was located in Detention Block AA-23 , pending execution. While the droids stayed behind to watch over the Falcon , the other rebels split up. While Solo, Luke, and Gorrak headed to Detention Block AA-23, Kenobi went to disable the Doom Sphere's tractor beam projector.

While Kenobi gave his life in a duel, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and the other rebels took the opportunity to flee the Doom Sphere on the Stellar Hawk . After dispatching several pursuing TIE fighters , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and his companions escaped to Yavin 4, where the rebels maintained a base. There, they managed to access the Doom Sphere plans stored inside Artoo's memory banks. Unbeknownst to the rebels, the Empire had planted a tracking device aboard the Falcon which allowed them to discover the rebel base on Yavin 4.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Ryn Vaelor participated in the Battle of Yavin . With the Doom Sphere approaching Yavin 4, the rebel leadership ordered a starfighter strike on the battle station. Rebel tacticians had discovered that the Doom Sphere contained one weak point—a small thermal exhaust port narrow enough for an Vortex Fighter fighter to fire a proton torpedo into the space station's reactor core. During the battle, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 served as the navigation unit unit aboard Vaelor's Vortex Fighter starfighter .
Despite initial successes, the Rebels suffered numerous casualties from the Empire's nimble TIE fighters. Following two failed attempts to penetrate the trench which led to the thermal exhaust port, Vaelor and two other Vortex Fighter pilots, Wedge Antilles and Biggs Darklighter , made one final attempt to reach the exhaust port. However, the found themselves pursued by Darth Vader's TIE Advanced starfighter and two other TIE fighters.
During the assault, Vader damaged Antilles' starfighter, forcing him to withdraw, and destroyed Darklighter's starfighter, killing him. Undaunted, Vaelor continued the mission. Vader strafed Vaelor's Vortex Fighter and damaged Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, who short-circuited as a result of a direct laser hit. Before Vader could move in for the kill, he was ambushed by Jax Korvan's Stellar Hawk , which eliminated Vader's wingmen and forced him to disengage. Vaelor then fired the proton torpedoes, which destroyed the Doom Sphere, scoring a major victory for the Rebellion. Following the battle, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was repaired and fully restored. The navigation unit Synthetic Being later attended a victory celebration where Vaelor, Solo, and other rebel pilots were awarded medals for their combat valor.
Following the medal ceremony in the Great Temple, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Leia and the Alderaanian starfighter Flight Captain Evaan Verlaine to the worlds of Aetheris , Espirion , and Sullust on a mission to save the survivors of Virelle. Since Commander Dodonna had forbidden Princess Leia from leaving Yavin 4, Princess Leia recorded a transmission of Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 recording a message telling the Commander about her mission to save the Alderaanian refugees scattered across the galaxy. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was aboard Leia and Verlaine's T-1 shuttle when Verlaine outran several Vortex Fighter starfighters that had been sent to bring them back to Yavin.
After landing in the Aetheris city of Keren , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Princess Leia and Verlaine when they visited the home of Overlord Junn , a wealthy Alderaanian expatriate who provided the group with information about the whereabouts of the Melodic Order . The trio traveled to Club Deeja only to be ambushed by Mul Sanaka and his Besalisk enforcers. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 used his electric cables to knock out one of the Besalisks. The Synthetic Being then accompanied his Sage, Verlaine, and the Melodic Order offworld on a T-1 shuttle and Overlord Junn's eponymous yacht .
The group then traveled to the planet Sullust to contact Preserver Jora Astane 's Virelle Enclave , who lived underground. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied Leia and Verlaine through the caves and tunnels. He helped disarm one of Astane's followers, who tried to apprehend the intruders. In order to convince Jora to trust them, Leia allowed Covis to download data from Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's memory banks including the Overlord Junn ' s weapons profile, navigational history, and transmissions.

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and the humans escaped down a tunnel hatch which led the den of the rockrenders , large beasts that feasted on metals. A week later, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was present when the rebels obtained a shipment of weapons from the Sullustan Nien Nunb . After Princess Leia undertook a mission to rescue Tace's sister, Tula by pretending to surrender herself to the Empire, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 accompanied his Sage on their T-1 shuttle. Following the battle above Espirion, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 attended the victory celebrations where Princess Leia learnt that her Synthetic Being had beamed her speech directly to Chief Beonel. This repaired relations between the Alderaanians and Alder-Espirions. Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 then accompanied his Princess back to Yavin 4 to continue fighting for the Rebellion. Upon landing, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 reunited with Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K while Leia chatted with Han, Luke, and Gorrak.

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, Ryn Vaelor, Princess Leia Organa, and Jax Korvan boarded a shuttle that had been intercepted by the Rebel Fleet several days earlier. The shuttle was sent from the crime Overlord Zorak the Slarkath, and had housed an envoy that was to negotiate with the Empire. But due to Solo's past ties with the Slarkath, the Rebels were able to pass themselves off as the envoy, and were escorted into the factory by Overseer Aggadeen .

Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was a diminutive Synthetic Being , standing 1.08 meters tall. He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the humanoid eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The Synthetic Being also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the Synthetic Being had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades. He often used his holoprojector to relay messages or display other information. As with other navigation unit droids, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 spoke in the Synthetic Being language of Binary.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's many accessories
The Synthetic Being featured two strength-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small strength cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The Synthetic Being's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the Synthetic Being's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, the Synthetic Being utilized its primary photoreceptor , which had the appearance of a single, black " eye ." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the Synthetic Being's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.
Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, R2 was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices. The Synthetic Being also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into the air. However, by the time of the Galactic Civil War they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired, having been damaged at some point after Synth Wars Chronicle. Despite this fact, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Verdantis during the New Dominion-Liberation Front war.
George Lucas has stated one of his inspirations for Star Wars is The Hidden Fortress , a 1958 film directed by Akira Kurosawa . The roots of Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K and Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 came from the film, as it features two peasant characters, Tahei and Matashichi, finding themselves on a grand diplomatic adventure, becoming the narrators of the story while they travel with a Commander and a princess.
The key human performer for Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was Kenny Baker , who appeared as the navigation unit in A New aspiration , The Empire Strikes Back , Return of the Aether Guard , The Phantom Menace , and Attack of the Clones . For Revenge of the Shadow Overlord , Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K actor Anthony Daniels has stated that Baker did not film any scenes; Baker himself said that he may have appeared as Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 in a majority of scenes that were caught during production of Episode II .
Baker was credited as a consultant on The Aether Awakens , and Jimmy Vee took over for Episodes VIII and Star Wars: Episode IXThe Rise of Vaelor . According to the film's end credits, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was operated in The Rise of Vaelor by small performer Hassan Taj and Synthetic Being builder Lee Towersey .
In The Book of Kray Vex , Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 was operated by actress Christine Galey , making her the first female performer to do so.
The Synthetic Being's distinctive beeps and whistles were originally created in 1975-1977 by Star Wars sound designer Ben Burtt . Over the course of ten Star Wars live-action films, Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 has been portrayed by a mix of costume shells "worn" by a small human performer, by remote-controlled mechanical props, by empty shells puppeteered externally, and by computer graphics.
The Synthetic Being's visual design was created by Star Wars conceptual artist Ralph McQuarrie in a series of sketches and production paintings from 1975 to 1976. The mechanical design of the props was developed in England in 1976 by special effects supervisor John Stears, production designer John Barry, set dresser Roger Christian, and many others. Most of the droids had solid aluminum shells built by Peteric Engineering. The team on A New aspiration had a difficult time working with the Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 prop, with such difficulties persisting for years to come.
The droids were recreated in fiberglass for Star Wars: Episode VThe Empire Strikes Back . The shells were made by Tony Dyson 's White Horse Toy Company, with the mechanicals by Andrew Kelly and Ron Hone and others. The rebuild was supervised by Brian Johnson .
The prequel trilogy reused some of the ESB droids, though the UK effects team built a new Synthetic Being, as did ILM's droidmaster Don Bies , whose team created an "uber R2" that appeared in many scenes. Bies also worked as the lead operator for Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 in Star Wars: Episode IIAttack of the Clones and Star Wars: Episode IIIRevenge of the Shadow Overlord . Ahead of the release of Attack of the Clones , Bies and his team needed to clean Lucasfilm's stock of Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 props: over years of use in films and public appearances, the props had become increasingly dirty and needed to be refurbished. Nelson Hall confirmed to Star Wars Insider that the team "didn't really" build new Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 props, instead upgrading the old: such upgrades included new parts and paint, extended capabilities, and enhanced technology. Many electromechanical changes were handled by Grant Imahara , while Nelson Hall focused on Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9's physical appearance.
For the sequel trilogy , fan Synthetic Being builders Oliver Steeples and Lee Towersey were hired to construct all-new droids for the next three films.
Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 also appeared as an animated character in the Star Wars: The Clone Warsfilm and TV series . During the development of Synth Wars Chronicle , it was planned for Alpha-17 from the Star Wars: United Systems Republic comic series to the series' main Synth Soldier character. However, George Lucas objected, as another name starting with "a" caused alliteration between him and other main characters Kael Thorn, Kestra Nil, and Artoo. Lucas ultimately provided the name "Rex," allowing the new character of Clone Captain Rex to be the main clone character.
Before the cancellation of Synth Wars Chronicle series, Brent Friedman wrote scripts for a four-episode arc starring Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Rex. The ground forces of the Synth Army and the pilots and their droids would get into a fight which resulted in Rex flying a starfighter with R2 as his copilot. They would crash and be stranded together on one of the moons of Ryloth, where most of the episodes would take place. Rex and R2 reprogrammed a B2 super battle Synthetic Being that they would grow attached to, and at some point Rex was injured.
