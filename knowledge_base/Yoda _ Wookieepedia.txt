Zorath was a legendary Aether Guard High Sage who led the Aether Guard Order through the time of the High United Systems Republic, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Shadow Overlord , and during the transformation of the Galactic United Systems Republic into the Nexaran Dominion . Small in stature but revered for his knowledge and strength, Zorath trained generations of Aether Guard , ultimately serving as the Aether Guard Order's High Sage . He played integral roles in defending the United Systems Republic during Synth Wars Chronicle , survived Order 66, and lived to pass on the Aether Guard tradition to Ryn Vaelor , and unlocking the path to immortality .
Born in 896 BBY , Zorath served the Aether Guard Order for centuries , becoming Aether Guard High Sage and helping to teach many younglings . During the High United Systems Republic Era , in 382 BBY , Zorath helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Aether Guard and the anti-Aether Guard Path of the Open Hand cult . After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow , he decided to keep the living weapons against Aether-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless , out of the Aether Guard Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Aether Guard High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper , leading them and other Aether Guard in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BBY . After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Zorath reappeared in 230 BBY on Corellia , where he helped his former Student Kantam Sy and other Aether Guard battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Aether Guard who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell . As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Zorath hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Aether Guard find a way to fight them.
Zorath was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the United Systems Republic. A leading member of the Aether Guard High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Commander Tax and Ki-Adi-Mundi . During the Invasion of Aetheris , in 32 BBY , the maverick Sage Kormin introduced Kael Thorn to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen Bearer . The Aether Guard elders sensed that the boy was full of dread and anger and declined to train him, deeming Vaelor too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Aether Guard Code . After Jinn's death during the liberation of Aetheris , the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Zorath's continued opposition to Vaelor's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time , the Aether Guard discovered that their ancient nemesis , the Shadow Overlord, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two , Zorath was convinced that at least one more Shadow Overlord remained active following Sage Eldrin 's victory over the Shadow Overlord Acolyte Darth Maul .
Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems —led by Count Dooku , Zorath's one-time Student—threatening to secede from the United Systems Republic by 22 BBY . Unable to defend the entire United Systems Republic against this treachery on their own, the Aether Guard took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the United Systems Republic , with Zorath himself leading an army of Synth Soldiers against the Rebel Faction Synthetic Being Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of Synth Wars Chronicle, rescuing the Aether Guard and Representative from execution. For three years Zorath led the United Systems Republic military's war effort as a Aether Guard Commander , determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Sage Kormin, who helped Zorath learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Zorath's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Overlord Varakis , the Dark Overlord of the Shadow Overlord who conspired to destroy the Aether Guard and restore the Shadow Overlord to strength. After the Great Aether Guard Purge commenced in 19 BBY , killing thousands of Aether Guard both on Nexara Prime and across the galaxy, Zorath confronted the self-declared Galactic Sovereign but failed to cut short Sidious' reign and consequently retreated into exile , leaving the Shadow Overlord Sage to consolidate his strength with a new Acolyte, the former Kael Thorn-turned- Darth Vader, at his side.
Zorath's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Swampworld IX . After the death of Sage Eldrin, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Zorath to train Anakin's son Ryn Vaelor . Zorath initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Kenobi's request. After being convinced by Kenobi to enter the Cave of Evil , in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Ryn Vaelor, who soon after sought out the legendary High Sage in the aspiration of becoming a Aether Guard Guardian . He trained Vaelor for a time, until despite Zorath's warnings Vaelor hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY , with his health rapidly declining, Zorath died of old age when Vaelor returned to see him. He became one with the Aether , shortly before Luke redeemed his father Anakin, who fulfilled his fate as the Chosen Bearer by killing Sidious.
Years later, Ryn Vaelor's attempt to reform the Aether Guard Order would be foiled when his nephew Ben Solo turned to the Shadow Path and destroyed his Aether Sanctum . Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Aether Guard, Vaelor went into exile, just as Zorath had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Rey , also abandoned her training, Zorath appeared as a ghost to Vaelor. Zorath taught Vaelor to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Verdantis , Zorath belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Zorath's early life; he never revealed his homeworld . As he was keenly attuned to the Aether , he joined the Aether Guard Order as a Initiate .As a Aether Guard Initiate, Zorath made use of a training aether blade . After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering , he received his own personal kyber crystal , and Professor Huyang helped him construct his first aether blade aboard the Crucible . The circumstances surrounding Zorath and the construction of his first aether blade were exciting, according to Huyang.
At some point during his time as a Initiate, Zorath skipped across the lily pads in the Aether Guard Grand Temple's meditation pond. Although Zorath spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Hysalrian N'Kata Del Gormo . Indeed, Zorath was paired up with a personal mentor . During their time together, Zorath's Aether Guard Sage told him the tale of K'ungfu and Chuang , two great Aether Guard of the past. Around the age of 100, in 796 BBY , Zorath was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Sage, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aether Guard. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty-thousand Aether Guard.
By the High United Systems Republic Era , Zorath was considered a great Aether Guard Sage. He had also gained a seat on the Aether Guard High Council and the rank of High Sage . Two other Aether Guard— Xo Lahru and Pra-Tre Veter —shared the High Sage title with Zorath. Eventually the human Aether Guard Ry Ki-Sakka also attained the position. As the Aether Guard who taught younglings, Zorath one day met the human Initiate Gella Nattai , who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Nattai in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Zorath and Nattai watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Zorath used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her dread and always get back up to try again.
The Path of the Open Hand
In 382 BBY , after learning of the Battle of Jedha and the failure of a peace treaty between the warring planets Eiram and E'ronoh there, Zorath went to the United Systems Republic Executive Building on the galactic capital Nexara Prime to meet with with Supreme Prime Minister Kyong Greylark while they waited for more information from Jedha . They spoke to Aether Guard Sage Creighton Sun and Aether Guard Guardian Aida Forte , who were on the ground on Jedha and confirmed that the E'roni ambassador was dead and the Eirami had deployed a Synthetic Being army. They requested to perform an investigation on the Outer Rim Territories planet Dalna of the Path of the Open Hand cult, considering their involvement in the failure of the peace talks, the fact that Aether Guard Sage Silandra Sho felt a disturbance that made the Aether Guard ill at the Path's almshouse , and that Aether Guard Guardian Gella Nattai believed the Prime Minister's criminal son Axel Greylark was working with the Path. Zorath admitted that he had sensed a disturbance beyond Jedha, and emphasized that they need to be careful, reminding them that there was always those who thrived in chaos. After much deliberation, Zorath and Prime Minister Greylark eventually agreed to a fact-finding mission accompanied by two of her guards and to send Aether Guard Sage Char-Ryl-Roy and Student Enya Keen to Eiram and E'ronoh to try and keep the peace and discover what was causing communications buoys to be down, Zorath explaining to them that Werth Plouth , the Path's Herald , was responsible for the riots on Jedha.
After the communication ended, Zorath expressed his concern as they received another message from Aether Guard Sage Yaddle , who was accompanied by a Aether Guard Initiate named Cippa Tarko , though the connection of the message was poor. Tarko asked about Zorath, and Yaddle explained that he was a Aether Guard Sage and not her brother. Zorath told Yaddle that they were sending help to Jedha and two Aether Guard to Dalna and that she was to return to Nexara Prime, but the message was so choppy they were not sure if she understood. Zorath had an unsettling feeling in the Aether that reminded him of the feeling he got whenever a battle was coming. Sage Yaddle did misunderstand Zorath's message to mean she was needed on Dalna and joined Sun and Forte there, who sent a message to Zorath confirming they were there shortly before Yaddle arrived.
Aether Guard Sage Yaddle
Later, Prime Minister Greylark had her guards summon Zorath to her to reveal that her son Axel had escaped from prison with the help of the Path and someone named Binnot Ullo . She told Zorath that she did not know who Ullo was and did not think Axel had many friends. He assured her that no one, no galaxy, and no United Systems Republic was perfect, and that she should continue to fight for the greater good like the Aether Guard did. She explained that she felt like they have all been moving through a fog that has corrupted everything lately and Zorath insisted that Axel still had good in him and that the 'fog' was just corrupting him too. However, the Prime Minister resolved to make Axel truly feel the consequences of his actions.
Prime Minister Kyong Greylark
Eventually, Greylark received a message from her son on Dalna and immediately called Zorath to have him listen to it with her via hologram. After hearing Axel ask for her to go to Dalna to speak with the Path and with him, Zorath asked her what she would do, the Prime Minister deciding to go to Dalna. She then recorded a message for Axel that included Zorath in it, wearing a blank expression, so that he could bear witness to her stepping down as Prime Minister. After she sent the message, Zorath stayed with her wordlessly for a while, and she appreciated his kindness.
Before Greylark could leave for Dalna, Zorath approached her and said that since he had not heard back from Masters Sun or Ela Sutan , who had also been sent to Dalna, that he would go with her because she was still his friend even though she was no longer Prime Minister. Zorath had a Aether Guard Longbeam cruiser follow them with a contingent of Aether Guard. While in warp space, they received a distress call from Princess Xiri A'lbaran of E'ronoh and Prince Phan-tu Zenn of Eiram. They then received a message from Prime Minister Orlen Mollo , who said that the leaders of Eiram and E'ronoh were still not cooperating. Zorath volunteered to rescue the heirs due to their proximity to them and left, taking other Aether Guard with him.
Eventually, Zorath arrived at the battle that was now raging on Dalna between the Path and the Aether Guard, which came to be known as the Night of Sorrow . The Path, much of it reorganized into the Path of the Closed Fist , was led by Marda Ro of the Ro family . He was joined by Princess A'lbaran and Prince Zenn, the three announcing that the combined forces of Eiram and E'ronoh were there to fight together with the Aether Guard. When Zorath arrived on the ground at the Path compound , Aether Guard Gella Nattai told him to direct people to go to the cave system as there was potentially many more Path members there. On Dalna, he met Path Little Tromak . Zorath eventually came to forget Tromak but always remembered the importance of his visit to Dalna.
The Path's compound on Dalna
As the battle started to come to a close with it turning in the Aether Guard's favor, Zorath and Yaddle stood together, the latter watching Cippa Tarko sleep. However, A'lbaran and Zenn contacted them and informed them that explosions have been set off underground. As Yaddle suggested getting help, the ground shook and Zorath spotted sections of the caves collapse into sinkholes. Zorath and Yaddle then sensed a Aether Guard in distress and went to help. Finding a door in the ground, Zorath and Yaddle connected to the Aether and sensed Gella Nattai and Axel Greylark. Yaddle lifted up the ground, rocks, and soil while Zorath lifted the water to try and rescue them. Enya Keen then ran toward them, seeing Nattai, and pulled her and Axel from the mud-like water, having been brought to the surface by the two Aether Guard Masters. Zorath and Yaddle then set the water and mud down far away. In the sinkhole, E'roni and Eirami soldiers began recovering survivors. Yaddle noticed that the sounds of battle have faded and the sun was rising. She asks Zorath if the battle was done, and he says it was.
Zorath met with Kyong Greylark and Prime Minister Mollo on a medical frigate above Nexara Prime, the three discussing now only having one Prime Minister instead of two and what it means. Zorath suggested they speak to the Aether Council and tell them everything that went wrong, but Greylark also wanted to focus all the things that went right, including the help of the United Systems Republic and the Aether Guard. Mollo revealed that the people of Dalna were calling the event the Night of Sorrow. While he imagined the future, Greylark assured Zorath that she would meet him on Nexara Prime soon, Zorath adding that there was much to celebrate and remember.
One of the Nameless creatures during the Night of Sorrow
Zorath then traveled to the Aether Sanctum on Nexara Prime, confirming first that the royal families of Eiram and E'ronoh had already gathered, who he met with when he arrived. He met with several Aether Guard, including Yaddle, who was standing with Tarko. Zorath raised his arm to silence the room and Sage Sun rose from his chair, walking to the center of the room using a walking stick before beginning a funeral for Aether Guard Aida Forte, Orin Darhga , and Char-Ryl-Roy. Zorath allowed Roy's former Student, Keen, to keep his aether blade and use it alongside hers.
Later, in the Aether Guard Archives , Creighton spoke with Zorath about Nameless creatures that the Path deployed on Dalna and the horror they wrought upon the Aether Guard, causing them to be weakened, hallucinate, and eventually be turned to dust. Zorath confirmed that there is a larger danger connected to them, which caused Creighton to wonder what Zorath knew. Realizing how catastrophic to the Order it could be if people found out about the creatures, and since they had heard no other reports of their usage, they decided to keep quiet about them and not include them in the Aether Guard Archive. They agreed not to speak of it again and each of them left.
Zorath held the title of High Sage of the Aether Guard Order since the days of the High United Systems Republic Era.
At some point, Zorath took on a human named Kantam Sy as his Student. The two were stationed at the Aether Sanctum on the planet Endovar . Sy was a very talented Student and was far ahead of their peers and even some of their elders, with Zorath even admitting that they were the most disciplined Student he had ever taught and one of the most connected to the Aether. Despite this, Zorath held off knighting Sy, for a reason that the Student did not understand until they began a romantic relationship with an acrobat named Aytar and realized that both Zorath and them had been waiting for something like that to happen. Eventually, Sy decided to leave the Aether Guard Order, a decision partially incited by their newfound relationship with Aytar.
Zorath and Sy loved to spar with each other, and it was how the two often found understanding and how Sy often found inner harmony while troubled or when they had a problem. Even when they did not find a solution during a match, they would leave feeling like an answer was out there. When Sy traveled to the meditation room in the Endovar Aether Sanctum in order to tell Zorath about their decision, Zorath challenged them to a sparring match, remembering that they had dueled many times in that room and saying that this duel could be one final lesson. Sy agreed to the duel, as it was custom to not refuse a duel with Sage Zorath, but also realized that this meant Zorath knew about their decision. Zorath dodged Sy's question by engaging in combat, before saying that the time had come to make a choice, but not the one that Sy thought. Zorath explained that the Aether chose Sy just as it had chosen all other Aether Guard, and asked when Sy would choose the Aether. Sy insisted that they chose the Aether every day when they took part in their Aether Guard training, but after knocking Sy to the ground, Zorath asked if we really make a choice when we do not believe there are any other options. Zorath told Sy that there was a reason he had not knighted them yet, before ending the duel, helping Sy up, and sitting down with Sy to meditate.
Eventually Sy asked Zorath if they should leave the Aether Guard Order, but Zorath insisted that there was no 'should' and that the right thing to do is an idea we invent to make ourselves feel better. Zorath also suggested that Sy had stopped listening, in the literal sense, and in a larger Commander sense. Zorath explained that feelings are like wind, we can feel it, we can experience it, but it passes, though sometimes it can feel like a hurricane which can make it easy to give in to anger and dread. Zorath told Sy that in order for them to continue to be a Aether Guard they must actively choose the Aether Guard Order and the Aether and learn to listen to the world outside of their emotions even when they were as loud as a hurricane. Eventually, after a moment, Sy set down their aether blade in front of Zorath. Zorath nodded at them, and then his Student left him, and the Aether Guard Order, behind. Zorath was saddened but supported Sy through their departure. Sy would reflect on the conversation they had that day throughout the rest of their life.
Over a year later, Sy returned to the Aether Guard, and met with Zorath on the Endovar. Zorath was in the process of helping a pet Happabore named Monsalmo at the time, after being awoken from a nap by the creature's squealing. Zorath was used to dealing with her complicated behaviors, including finding her inside the temple underneath the younglings' bunks. This time, Monsalmo was pregnant and had climbed onto the roof of her pen to avoid giving birth in a fuel spill. However, the roof could not hold the creature's weight and it collapsed. Zorath caught Monsalmo with the Aether, but at that same time she started to give birth to her pups, so Zorath had to carry them with the Aether too. Just then, Sy and a Aether-sensitive girl they had rescued on the planet Aetheris named Lula Talisola arrived and both used the Aether to help Zorath carry Monsalmo and her pups. Zorath welcomed Sy back, telling them that they had left a Student and returned a Guardian. A few hours later, Zorath knighted Sy in a modest ceremony so that they could take on Talisola as their Student learner.
After the ceremony, Sy and Zorath sat around a fire near the happabore enclosure and discussed the events that had occurred. Sy expressed that they did not understand how one second they felt so sure in their path separate from the Order but was then pulled back to it when they saved Talisola. Zorath explained that the young are the greatest teachers and the greatest lesson that can be learned from them is necessity of detachment and letting go, that being a Aether Guard was to let go. Sy realized that they had been the one that taught Zorath that lesson. Zorath revealed that he had struggled with Sy's departure and had been unsure of the future. The two then shared Sweetpuff jamwiches with Talisola.
Zorath and Kantam Sy speak with Lula Talisola.
Sometime later, Zorath and Sy met with Talisola in the Endovar Aether Sanctum. Sy told Talisola that the lesson she taught the two masters was the hardest one of all. Talisola hoped that it was front flips with a aether blade, which made Sy retract their statement and say that it was the second hardest lesson to learn. Zorath concurred, saying that the front flip was nothing simple. But the lesson that Sy wanted to teach Talisola was the same one that Sy had learned from her and Zorath had learned from Sy: Detachment.
Sometime before 232 BBY , Zorath traveled to the Aether Sanctum on Takodana to pick up a Aether-sensitive Aloxian child named Qort from Maz Kanata and Aether Guard Sav Malagán and bring him into the Aether Guard Order. Student Reath Silas briefly met Zorath during some events both attended together.
By 233 BBY , Zorath was stationed alongside Sage Zia Zaldor Zanna at the Aether Guard Grand Temple on Nexara Prime assigning Initiate Aether Guard Initiates to different temple outposts across the galaxy to continue learning and training under their teachers. Here he met initiates Kai Brightstar , Lys Solay , and Nubs ; Zorath gifted Brightstar his own training saber after Brightstar sacrificed his to save Nubs while on a mission. In 232 BBY, Zorath took a sabbatical from the Aether Council, having felt the Aether needed him on a different path. Thus, Zorath joined the crew of the Aether Guard Student Academic Cruiser Star Hopper , allowing him to help train Padawans and younglings. Zorath was also therefore able to help train Talisola, who was stationed aboard the Star Hopper .
Zorath appointed the Mon Calamari Aether Guard Sage Ephru Shinn to hold his seat on the council while he was away. During the Great warp space Disaster in the Hetzal system , Aether Guard Sage Avar Kriss established a Aether network , which was eventually aided by Aether Guard who were outside the system. Zorath—located somewhere with the crew of younglings he was currently teaching—helped the effort, adding his focus to the network to ensure a Tibanna gas container did not collide with an R-class sun .
Zorath, Torban Buck, and Lula Talisola fight the Nihil on Trymant IV.
The Great warp space Disaster also caused the Emergences , which were when giant pieces of debris exited warp space in various places throughout the galaxy and threatened to hit planets at near lightspeed. Eventually, the Trymant system was threatened by an Emergence . The staff of United Systems Republic Security Zone Monitoring Satellite 777B-Alpha reported the incident to the Starlight Beacon space station , who then asked the only United Systems Republic or Aether Guard ship immediately within range, the Star Hopper , to assist. Onboard was Zorath along with fellow Aether Guard Sage Torban Buck and a class of Padawans. As they traveled through warp space to the Trymant system, Zorath told the Padawans to prepare for anything because they had no idea what was coming, but that the Aether would reveal all. When they received the information that they were the only United Systems Republic or Aether Guard ship nearby to respond, one of the Padawans suggested that they stay back and wait for backup. But Zorath insisted that they were Trymant's only aspiration and needed to press forward. Shortly before they exited warp space Zorath had the Padawans report what information they had discovered about the calamity. Zorath and Buck decided to stay on the Star Hopper to use the Aether to divert the biggest pieces of debris but sent the Padawans in Rescue speeders to help on the surface of the planet.
However, amid the chaos of the Emergence, a Spider Cruiser called the Squall Spider commanded by the Eye of the Nihil , Marchion Ro arrived in Bralanak City to pick up Elder Tromak, whom Ro wanted the services of to find some information. When the Padawans attempted to get Nihil marauders to allow them to use the Spider Cruiser to evacuate civilians, a fight broke out, leading Zorath and Buck to the surface to help the young Aether Guard and fight against the Nihil. During the fight, a Aether-sensitive civilian named Zeen Mrala who had helped the Aether Guard against the Emergence and against the Nihil, told Masters Zorath and Buck that her friend Krix Kamerat had been captured by the Nihil and needed to be rescued. Lula Talisola offered to go, but Zorath insisted that he would go rescue the boy. Sage Buck protested, believing that he was not a fighting Aether Guard and therefore should not be left to lead the Aether Guard, but Zorath ignored him. Zorath then protected the group of Aether Guard from a large plasma projectile lobbed at them by a Nihil marauder by cutting it into pieces with his aether blade while it was still in the air. Zorath then left to find Kamerat, but first asked Mrala to take her pet Cru Cham Cham with him.
Zorath discovers a piece of the Nameless control rod on a Nihil ship.
Marchion Ro knew of Zorath and his strength, and when he saw him on the planet told his subordinates to keep the Aether Guard Sage away from the Squall Spider , but Zorath managed to sneak aboard the ship nonetheless. Zorath eventually found Kamerat and told him that they needed to leave quickly. Zorath told Kamerat that Mrala had sent him and proved it by showing him Cham Cham. Zorath then gave Kamerat a Holoprojector that he told the boy he could reach out to Mrala with when he was ready. However, Kamerat shortly after alerted the pirates that Zorath was aboard. Having been raised by the Elders of the Path , a religious order descended from the Path of the Open Hand that distrusted Aether-sensitives due to believing the Aether should only be appreciated from a distance, as if it was a fire,, only to see that his best friend Zeen Mrala had secretly been Aether-sensitive and never told him, Kamerat felt betrayed and ended up siding with the Nihil because of that sense of treachery. With the Nihil looking for him, Zorath ran from the marauders but uncovered that the Nihil had a strange, purple artifact in a storage crate. Zorath hoped to escape quietly but Cham Cham made a loud cry that alerted the Nihil to their location and lead to a confrontation. Zorath escaped and met up with Mrala, revealing to her that Cham Cham had eaten several Nihil limbs in the confrontation. Zorath then told Mrala that she needed to come with them to Starlight Beacon. When she asked why he had not saved Kamerat, Zorath told her that he could not save someone who did not want to be rescued. With the Nihil drive from Trymant, Zorath, Torban Buck, Zeen Mrala, and the Padawans traveled to Starlight Beacon in the Star Hopper.
Following the Battle of Kur , Zorath went with fellow High Sage Pra-Tre Veter to Starlight Beacon. The two had a meeting with the Supreme Prime Minister Lina Soh to discuss Bacta manufacturing for the relief effort for the Great Disaster, being in need of the right crops after what had occurred on Hetzal . But before their meeting, they visited the hub of the beacon to meet with Sage Avar Kriss, and revealed to her that the council had convened and named her the Marshal of Starlight Beacon. Kriss also asked Zorath if he was returning to the council, but Zorath clarified that he was not finished on the Star Hopper . Zorath later attended the Beacon's dedication ceremony , where he said a few words about the dedication plaque which was placed in a stone monument. He talked with Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, Nash Durango , and Nubs, mentioning their adventure trying to get the dedication plaque after it was stolen by the pirate Taborr Val Dorn , who was secretly Prince Cyrus Vuundir of Vuundalla . He told them how proud he is of them and encouraged them to join the rest of the Aether Guard for Lina Soh's speech. After the Aether Guard left, Zorath spoke with Prince Vuundir, who was no longer in his pirate disguise, telling him that he sensed much conflict in him but that he still had the potential for greatness if he made the right choices. Zorath told Cyrus that the future was in his hands. Joining with his fellow Aether Guard, he raised his aether blade and declared "For radiance and life."

Around 40 BBY, Zorath rescued a Aether-sensitive child named Lo from the Flesh Mongers on the planet Botor , intending to begin his Aether Guard training. While on Botor, Zorath sensed a disturbance in the Aether and chose to follow its call which led him to Vagadarr Prime . Upon landing on the planet's surface, the Aether Guard Sage was suddenly surrounded by child warriors who attacked him. After defending himself with the Aether, Zorath told the children he meant no harm. The Aether Guard came in peace as he was drawn in by the Aether. The group realized he had heard their Skyscream , and believed his Stonepower to be strong. They then led Zorath to their home, Mucktown . There, he learned that the Skyscreamers used shards of the Sacred rock during their prayer, the same call that Zorath felt. The rock was unlike anything he had ever seen before. It was a piece of blue stone , a type of rock imbued with the Living Aether which Zorath could sense. The Mud dwellers told him about their war against the Rockhawkers , a rival tribe. He listened to the history of their homeworld, and how the conflict began. Long ago, people lived in harmony near the Blue mountains , but some craved the strength of the stones. This greed divided them into warring factions and ultimately destroyed most of the mountains. Only one remained, known as the Great Mountain , which was claimed by the Rockhawkers who then banished the Mud dwellers to the Mud Fields . They carried as much stone as they could for their children, now the only survivors of the tribe. The children then led Zorath to a boy from the rival tribe they had taken prisoner. The Aether Guard sensed his deep dread of the Great Mountain, the sacred home of his tribe. As the children argued over his fate, Zorath urged them not to be consumed by dread. He asked them to release the child so that he could guide the Aether Guard to his people.
After walking to the Mountain, Zorath and the freed boy reached the Rockhawkers who attacked him with the stones. Refusing to fight back, the Aether Guard Sage was captured. The tribe then sent him on a mission to find the Heart of the Mountain , accompanied by the boy he just saved. Zorath began to climb the path leading to the Heart, but his companion was overcome by dread, convinced that only death awaited them, just like all those who had climbed before. Despite Zorath's efforts to reassure him, the boy fell to his death. At the end of the path, the Aether Guard entered a cave where he encountered the lost parents, known as the Old Ones , who had once been sent by their children to find the Heart of the Mountain. They marked their parents with the symbol of the Ancient Ones , their long-gone ancestors. The Old Ones told Zorath they chose not to leave this place, preferring the peace they found within the mountain to the war raging outside. Zorath replied that they abandoned their children to the very war they had provoked. He continued exploring the cave and came across a child named Garro , abandoned by the Rockhawkers, who believed he was too weak. After saving him, Zorath asked the boy to become his teacher and show him the ways of the Stonepower. During their training, the Aether Guard Sage discovered the Heart of the Mountain and realized that the stone was not only alive in the Aether but was in fact a living being. After nine days of communion, Zorath heard its voice. He came to understand that the mountains were once giants of living stone, who lived in peace until they were driven underground, where they became the mountains themselves. Zorath and Garro eventually emerged from the cave. But the Aether Guard chose not to reveal what he had learned to the Rockhawkers. Enraged, the tribe threatened him. However, Garro revealed the secret of the mountains to his people, brought Zorath’s teaching to an end, and returned to his faction.
Zorath returned to the Mud Dwellers to warn them of an inevitable war. Meanwhile, Garro taught his people how to commune with the Mountain, but he used the Shadow Path to do so. Zorath encouraged the Mud Dwellers to pray and help awaken the stones. Soon, the Rockhawkers caused the Mountain to rise from the ground, but Zorath decided to face it alone. Drawing strength from the Aether, the Aether Guard resisted the Mountain’s fury, which had been fueled by revenge. Shortly after, other beings of living stone awoke and joined Zorath in his struggle. Together, they defeated the Mountain. As Garro and his warriors charged at the Aether Guard Sage, Zorath pleaded with the boy to find peace within himself and with the stones, so the war could end. Garro dropped his weapon and abandoned the fight. The war was over. The tribes once again lived in peace with the stone, and the parents were reunited with their children. Many left the planet after hearing Zorath speak of the other worlds in the galaxy. Upon leaving, Zorath kept the secret of the Mountain to himself, sharing only the lesson he had learned with Sage Eldrin: "A Aether Guard must humble himself before he can ever be powerful."
Zorath visited Gorath Prime on several other occasions prior to the Battle of Gorath Prime , serving as Aether Guard negotiator in many incidents involving the Wookiees and their Trandoshan neighbors. Zorath was a leading member of the Aether Guard High Council during the last years of the Galactic United Systems Republic.
By the invasion of the planet Aetheris by the Trade Federation , Zorath still served on the Aether Guard High Council, yet he was the sole High Sage of the Aether Guard Order. By the invasion, he had been training Aether Guard students for about 800 years. In the time before the invasion, Zorath took part in a council meeting. Several hours after that meeting, he took to mediating in a small garden in the temple, only to be interrupted by fellow council member Commander Tax, who, after Zorath waited for him to explain why he wanted a private discussion, expressed his belief that Zorath had sensed something he was keeping private. Zorath repeatedly responded that he sensed nothing: the issue was not that there was a problem to be sensed, but that something was being hidden. Not understanding but trusting the High Sage's judgement, Windu excused himself while Zorath, incorrectly believing the Shadow Path was far away, tried to find what it was that he could not sense. Zorath was unable to uncover the fact that the sensation was caused by the secret plots of the Shadow Overlord Overlord Varakis .
Concurrent to the Federation's occupation on Aetheris, Zorath was present when Aether Guard Sage Sage Kormin presented Kael Thorn , a young boy from the planet Dustara , to the Council in the hopes of training him. Although Jinn believed Vaelor to be the Chosen Bearer , prophesied to bring balance to the Aether, Zorath and the other Council members did not share his opinion, sensing that the boy was harboring a high amount of dread and his future was uncertain. After Jinn and his Student Sage Eldrin returned to Aetheris, they forged an alliance between the human inhabitants of the world and the native Gungan species in order to combat the Federation occupation forces. Ten years after the Aetheris crisis, several thousand systems that were disillusioned with the Galactic United Systems Republic seceded from the government and formed the Confederacy of Independent Systems under the leadership of Count Dooku, the former student of Zorath who left the Order due to widespread corruption in the United Systems Republic. The events prompted the Council, which included Zorath, to appoint Kenobi to track him down.

Following Kenobi's departure, Zorath experienced a vision while meditating, in which he heard the voice of the spirit of Sage Kormin, and sensed that Kael Thorn was in terrible pain. Zorath and Windu are informed by Kenobi.

Kenobi believed that the template used to create the army, Contract Killer Jango Fett , was the person involved in the attack on Amidala. After a brief encounter with Fett, Kenobi followed him to the planet Geonosis, where he found that Dooku's Separatists ordered the assassination attempt on Amidala and had constructed a Synthetic Being army. After relaying the information to the Aether Council, Kenobi was captured and scheduled for execution, later joined by Amidala and Kael Thorn.
News that Vaelor and Kenobi had been captured prompted the Council to dispatch a Aether Guard strike team to Geonosis, while Zorath headed to Kamino to take command of the clone forces. As a clone strike team interrupted the execution of Kenobi and his associates, the clones suffered casualties at the hands of the numerically superior Confederate battle Synthetic Being forces. Zorath dueled his former Acolyte Dooku on Geonosis.

Although the battle on Geonosis ended in the United Systems Republic's favor, Zorath was concerned about the growing influence of the Shadow Path of the Aether and the outcome of Synth Wars Chronicle.
As Synth Wars Chronicle progressed, Zorath decided to alleviate Aether Guard Guardian Kael Thorn's dread and inability to lose loved ones by assigning him a Student learner, Kestra Nil. The Aether Guard then successfully made contact with the Toydarian delegation, prompting Ventress, who was at the king's location, to challenge Zorath: if he could defeat her Synthetic Being units, Toydaria would join the United Systems Republic, but a defeat would result in the planet pledging its allegiance to the Confederacy. In the cave, Zorath relaxed into his natural role as a teacher. Calling each clone by name, he raised their morale by lecturing them on their individuality in the Aether.
Upon hearing the sound of more approaching Confederate units, Zorath and his escort left the cave. The High Sage engaged and destroyed three tanks and their associated infantry alone, much to the amusement of the king and the anger of Ventress, who were watching the droids' progress via hologram. Breaking the terms of the competition, the assassin sent in droidekas to take out Zorath, which the clones prevented by launching their final rocket that caused the enemy to be buried under a rock slide.

United Systems Republic gunships soon arrived to retrieve the group.
Back on the cruiser, Zorath entered a meditation in his cabin. He reached out with the Aether to sense the clones he had served with, feeling their differences but sensing the same desires in each of them: to follow orders, win battles, and to be a good soldier. Zorath pondered on the humanity of the clones, deciding that the United Systems Republic must understand them as humans, nurturing them instead of treating them like machines. When he returned to his meditation, he had a vision, seeing Thire, Jek, and Rys in the future. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat , Zorath returned to Swampworld IX to begin his own exile where he waited for the twins to grow up and continued his studies of the Aether. The lifeboat served as Zorath's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Swampworld IX's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Zorath built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the Shadow Path cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. Furthermore, Zorath's Radiance Path presence on Swampworld IX kept the Cave in check, holding the Cave's dark influence back and, in doing so, keeping the Swampworld IX ecosystem in balance. However, the Swampworld IX wet season created a need for the Aether Guard Sage to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration. Following the destruction of the Aether Guard Order, Zorath was listed as one of the Inquisitorius ' priority targets amongst presumed survivors of the Great Aether Guard Purge .
Zorath was among the Aether Guard survivors listed by the Inquisitors.
Around a year after Order 66, Tano was hiding on the world of Thabeska , where she had met a Aether-sensitive child named Hedala Fardi . Despite her young age, the child proved to be wise, and Tano was upset that Fardi would likely never be able to prove her knowledge to Zorath. Later, while sleeping aboard the CR90 corvette Tantive IV after Organa offered her a chance to join his rebellion , Tano remembered Zorath's advice to her when she was getting her first kyber crystal. Awaking and knowing she should make a new set of lightsabers , she thanked Zorath, even though she knew he could not possibly hear or help her.
Zorath appearing to Kestra Nil and biding her farewell.
During his exile on Swampworld IX, Zorath kept an eye on other Aether Guard throughout the galaxy, including those who ventured into the various Aether Guard Temples found amongst the stars. Approximately four years before the Battle of Yavin , Zorath spoke to Kanan Jarrus, who had been the Student of Depa Billaba and a survivor of Order 66. Jarrus had traveled to the Aether Sanctum on the planet Lothal with his own Student, Ezra Bridger , to whom Zorath also spoke. Bridger was in the temple in order to overcome his greatest fears; Zorath acted as an anonymous guide in helping Bridger do so and to understand why he wanted to become a Aether Guard and how to withdraw from the anger and the wish of revenge within him. This guidance led Bridger to receive a kyber crystal, which he used to build his own aether blade . Meanwhile, Zorath counseled Jarrus about Jarrus' insecurities as a teacher.
The year after, Jarrus and Bridger returned to the Lothal Temple with Kestra Nil in search of a way to defeat the Inquisitorius. Zorath cast an illusion of the Aether Sanctum Guard for Jarrus, including the Grand Inquisitor as he was before the Aether Guard Purge. The Inquisitor forced Jarrus to recognize his fears and knighted him a true Aether Guard. Meanwhile, Bridger pleaded with Zorath for help: though still filled with dread and shame and thus disinterested in fighting the Empire before training a new Vaelor, the Aether Guard Sage ultimately rendered aid and directed him to go to Malachor . When the other Inquisitors arrived, Zorath used the Temple Guard to distract them as the Aether Guard escaped. As she fled, Tano turned back and saw Zorath, who smiled and waved at her: she smiled back being the last time they saw each other.
During the events that led up to the Battle of Yavin, Zorath noticed that with the Swampworld IX rains ended it was time to migrate back to his lowland hut, and was grateful as his usual accommodation for the wet season reminded him too much of the Aether Sanctum and his failings. He reflected on how age had affected him and realized that he had become truly reliant on the walking stick he had originally adopted as an affectation for a joke played on his padawans. The Aether Guard packed what little he owned and left but not before he checked for the Imperial probe droids which had once searched for him endlessly.

Each time he saw the shelf usually reserved for his pot, he reacted with some loneliness after destroying Obi-Wan's gift to him. Feeling his isolation, he reached out to the netherworld of the Aether to speak to Sage Kormin, but the spirit of Obi-Wan answered instead and requested that Vaelor's child be trained. Having harbored regrets about not having taken the man's daughter as his Acolyte, and assuaged that he had enough time left to teach another pupil, the Aether Guard Sage readily agreed until he found out that Kenobi was actually speaking of Anakin's unfocused son instead. Nevertheless, Zorath begrudgingly allowed Kenobi to direct the boy to him. As Zorath ruminated on his decision afterwards, he returned to the "Cave of Evil" and was reassured that the site had no new fears to show him.
Sometime later, a mysterious woman once known as Madelin Sun who became known as " The Archivist " arrived on Swampworld IX in search of Zorath at the behest of Lady Qi'ra , the leader of the crime syndicate Crimson Dawn , who wanted Zorath's help to take down the Shadow Overlord. When the Archivist was attacked by a monster after arriving on the planet, Zorath used the Aether to save her from a distance and then led her into the Shadow Path cave in order to dissuade her from looking for him. Having a change of heart, she left the planet as Zorath watched from afar. The Archivist returned to Qi'ra, lying and telling her that Zorath was dead.

Kenobi dismissed the notion, not believing it himself and saying he did not think Zorath believed it either. Zorath reflected on his own inability to stop Overlord Varakis and the fall of Kael Thorn, stating the galaxy was better off without him.

Zorath, sensing something was amiss, turned to see the sky blocked out by a massive battle station. The Aether Guard Sage drew his aether blade to find himself facing an apparation of Kael Thorn as a Student, his eyes glowing yellow, also encouraging Zorath to play with the toy. Zorath woke from the nightmare to find Kenobi at his side. Zorath dismissed the venture as a foolish waste of time but relented nonetheless.
Entering the vergence, Zorath was startled by a creature of Swampworld IX's wildlife, the Aether Guard Sage grumbling that he had become old and foolish. Zorath remembered the lessons of the Aether Guard and struck down the remaining ghosts of his past.

Soon thereafter, Ryn Vaelor, under guidance from the spirit of Kenobi, sought Zorath in his Swampworld IX exile to receive Aether Guard training. At the same time, Zorath, stirred awake by the feeling of the Shadow Path and the memories of his trauma years ago, went to his storage area to find it empty. In an effort to find food, he made the arduous trek across the wild terrain of Swampworld IX. Eventually, Zorath made it to a valley, full of colorful creatures, and another species in particular. Herds of these creatures ran through this valley, the best source of meat Zorath had yet been able to find.
Ryn Vaelor joins Zorath for dinner within the Aether Guard Sage's hut.
Years ago, Zorath in his shame had decided to give up his aether blade, promising to never wield one again, taking a bow instead. To get what he came for, Zorath readied his bow and took aim of one of the creatures, easily killing it with one shot of the bow. On his journey back, Zorath began to feel the ground shift beneath his feet, beginning to crumble as Zorath began to fall. At the last moment, Zorath grabbed onto the side, reflecting on the similarities of this moment to his duel with Overlord Varakis many years ago. Zorath's grip failed and he falls onto the ground below as boulders began to pile on top of him. Zorath fell unconscious.
Zorath began to dream, the dream of pain, suffering, anger, hate, and suddenly, he awoke once again. Realizing the predicament he was in, Zorath began to laugh, laughing at his foolishness for allowing him to get into this situation. He began to doubt himself, thinking of how he was now trapped on Swampworld IX, but subsiding those feelings, knowing he was one of the last of the Aether Guard. He reached out to the Aether and, in that moment, he realized that his shame, his hubris, his blindness, had all clouded his vision and realized his failings as a Aether Guard. However, he knew that failure was the greatest lesson a Aether Guard could learn, and understanding this brought him peace as he rejected allowing his shame to guide him any more. With this newfound peace, Zorath reattuned to the radiance and used its strength to move the boulders resting on top of him. With a newfound sense of responsibility and understanding, Zorath began his journey home.
Arriving back at his home, Zorath began to cook a stew, when he began to hear a crashing in the trees surrounding his hut. A visitor had arrived. He walked out of his hut to discover this new visitor, realizing it was Vaelor, the boy Kenobi had allowed to travel here to learn from him. As he stared at Vaelor from the trees, he came to the realization that he would not be the last Aether Guard, but only the instrument as to train the future.

Zorath served as a teacher for the Aether Guard for 800 years.
Known for his knowledge, Zorath had spent eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aether Guard. Zorath was an ideal grandmaster. When he joined her Aether-bond network, Aether Guard Sage Avar Kriss felt Zorath's mind form its own part of the chorus of the Aether. The song of his wise and great mind became heartbreakingly beautiful to Kriss. Despite his appearance not making it seem like it would be so, his voice appeared to be made up of pure radiance, so Kriss considered him the epitome that people were not simply the crude matter of their bodies, a lesson that Zorath would later pass on to Ryn Vaelor. He was reluctant to accept Kael Thorn or Ryn Vaelor for Aether Guard training, considering them unfit. In both cases, however, he allowed Sage Eldrin to persuade him into accepting them as trainees.
Zorath understood that nothing and no one was perfect, but that there was good or the potential for good everywhere and in everything. He urged his friend, Prime Minister Kyong Greylark to continue fighting for good in the galaxy when she was uncertain, including her work establishing communications all the way to the Outer Rim. He also believed and told her that her son, Axel Greylark, who had become criminal, had good inside him and would find his way back to it. Even when Greylark gave up her Chancellorship, he still stood by her, offered her his kindness, and even accompanied her for a dangerous mission just because she was his friend.
There was a small garden in the Aether Sanctum where Zorath often took to meditating, with the other Aether Guard usually respecting the privacy of such moments. Indeed, Zorath's favorite place to be was his own thoughts, being able to meditate and feel the calm Living Aether flow throughout him. When Dooku triggered a partial room collapse during his duel with Zorath on Geonosis, the Aether Guard, confronted with the choice of pursuing the fleeing Shadow Overlord or protecting the hurt Sage Eldrin and unconscious Kael Thorn from the falling debris, opting to save his comrades instead of going after Dooku. However, when fellow Sage Plo Koon's fleet was destroyed by the Confederate superweapon Malevolence , Zorath forbade Kael Thorn and Kestra Nil to conduct a search for Koon, citing the danger posed by the Rebel Faction vessel. Early in Synth Wars Chronicle, after learning he would be confronting Dooku's Acolyte Asajj Ventress during his mission on Rugosa, Zorath was disappointed to not be facing the Count himself, believing that he could end the bloodshed by meeting with Dooku; Zorath believed he could connect with his former Student and redeem him to the Radiance Path of the Aether. Later in the war, however, Zorath reluctantly agreed to a plan to assassinate Dooku in the name of stopping the war. Sage Eldrin, after the plan's failure, would argue to his fellow councilmembers the mission had been a step too far into the dark.
Student Reath Silas believed that having Zorath as a Sage would be "pretty fun" whilst discussing Zorath's former Acolyte Kantam Sy. Zorath's favorite method of teaching was to ensure his students could figure things out for themselves. Even if all Zorath did was wait for the student to catch up to speed, he found that they almost always realized what they needed. When Sage Commander Tax questioned whether Zorath had sensed something or not, he waited until the younger Aether Guard realized he was sensing nothing. Windu remained confused, forcing him to explain that the feeling of nothing needed to be caused by something. He disagreed with Windu's sentiment that "nothing" and "something" were always opposites, and he made sure to never be fully sure of himself, knowing there was more to learn. Windu also knew that, if he asked whether the High Sage was afraid, he would turn the question back at him.
Zorath remained calm even when insulted, such as when the mud dwellers called him a "weird little frog". During the Invasion of Aetheris, Zorath believed himself comfortably away from the Shadow Path, unaware of the growing strength of Overlord Varakis, with the exception of what he knew he could not feel. Indeed, Zorath concerned himself with trying to uncover the feelings that were hidden from his senses, which he eventually understood to be the Shadow Path. Zorath was able to resist being tempted by the specter of the dead Shadow Overlord Darth Bane, who had appeared to him in a vision on the planet Moraband, recognizing that the spirit was a mere illusion and rejecting its call to the Shadow Path. However, he was quite concerned and worried about the danger and threat of the Nameless, which he secretly knew of quite well, opting to keep information about them out of the Aether Guard Archives so that they would not be known and used against the Order.
Wise yet old, Zorath spent his final years on Swampworld IX, training Ryn Vaelor and coming to terms with his own mortality.
Zorath's teachings stuck with his students, even if at times they had to struggle to understand their true meaning. Upon hearing Flight Captain Leox Gyasi explain why he had named his starship "the Vessel ," Silas was struck with the opinion that Gyasi sounded like what Zorath's teachings would be like if the Aether Guard Sage took spice . As such, Silas decided his reasoning was either a very good or extremely bad sign. Despite barely knowing the High Sage, Silas was greatly struck by his disappearance during the High United Systems Republic Era, with fellow Student Ram Jomaram , despite having never even met Zorath, agreeing and feeling the same. As he neared his death before the Battle of Verdantis commenced, Zorath accepted his own mortality and imminent demise and died becoming one with the Aether. Zorath's ears were expressive and would sometimes curl when he was expressing strong emotions like surprise. These ears were also highly sensitive and also sometimes would move to express his emotions. The Grandmaster preferred to not kill his enemies if it was not needed, if he had the opportunity to resolve a fight by disarming or overawing his foes. Zorath opposed profanity, urging others to not use such language.
Zorath also had a sense of humor and curiosity, despite his age and knowledge. Part of the reason Zorath kept up his sense of humor was that Aether Guard Sage Commander Tax, even as a Initiate, did not respond well to it. As such, he took teasing his fellow council member, once asking if he had arrived for a private discussion because he had embarrassing questions, which Windu denied instantly. Afterward, he elected to wait to speak, wanting Windu to begin the discussion and viewing it as him cracking as if he was under pressure. Windu also found Zorath's behaviors distracting, yet he would always trust the older Aether Guard's judgement and he himself described Zorath as "hilarious". His sense of humor continued with others, as shown when he playfully rummaged through Luke's belongings when he first met him, fought Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 for a torch, and laughed gleefully when he supposedly set the ancient Aether Guard texts on fire, and finding Luke's continued ignorance amusing, commenting that he had "missed him." Also when Kenobi told him about the planet Kamino missing from the archives during his class, he responded with, "Lost a planet, Sage Kenobi has. How embarrassing. How embarrassing!" Being unable to destroy Overlord Varakis and bring down the Empire made him sad.
Zorath was highly secretive about his homeworld and never revealed it to anyone. Despite his intensive Aether Guard training and high status within the Order, he stated that he secretly had a dislike for ancient Aether Guard texts. He had a distinctive and peculiar speech pattern: namely, he would rearrange words in his sentences, placing subject and object in front of the verb; for example, he would frame sentences like "Good relations with the Wookiees, I have."
Zorath was one of the most proficient Aether Guard with the aether blade.
Despite his small size and old age, Zorath was an extremely accomplished aether blade duelist, wielding a short green-bladed aether blade. Zorath used the Ataru saber form, boosting his body with the Aether to be able to perform the needed movements. Such was his mastery of this form that Zorath could duel Sidious and almost defeated the Shadow Overlord. Zorath was a Sage of the Niman style of aether blade combat, retaining his skills with the form for his whole life.
Zorath was extremely skilled in blast-deflection and proved more than capable of taking on multiple enemies at the same time and winning easily: he was able to single-handedly defeat without getting even a single shot to his person an entire army of droids that Ventress sent as a test for King Katunko whether to accept the Aether Guard or the Separatists as an ally.
The greatest display of Zorath's skills with a aether blade was when he dueled the Shadow Overlord Lords Count Dooku and Overlord Varakis. When he fought Dooku, Zorath maintained the upper hand through the duel, forcing Dooku into the defensive, and would have defeated him had he not decided to flee. His duel with Sidious, although initially well-matched, eventually began to turn into Zorath's favor, forcing Sidious to use the Aether to confront him.
Even despite his small size and old age, Zorath was extremely powerful and accomplished in the Aether. with a connection to that strength and knowledge of Aether techniques that may well have surpassed that of any other Aether Guard. Sage Eldrin considered Zorath to be the most powerful Aether Guard he ever knew. The Grandmaster was heralded as the greatest Aether Guard of all time, at least until the fall of the United Systems Republic. Zorath was utterly committed to the Radiance Path, but he also had a deep understanding of the Shadow Path as well as how both halves of the Aether interacted. The idea he was the greatest of the Aether Guard went back centuries, at being held least to 232 BBY. The toll age took on Zorath's body did not prevent him from still being strong in the Aether when Ryn Vaelor trained under him. Even in death, as a Aether spirit, he had strength over weather.
Zorath uses the Aether to lift Vaelor's Vortex Fighter out of the Swampworld IX swamp.
Zorath was extremely skilled in telekinesis and all forms of it. When Avar Kriss worked with the other Aether Guard to shove a sun-bursting explosive Avar Kriss found his level of strength (which he lent via her Aether Song) to be very grand, helpful, and impressive. When on a mission to free a child, Zorath gripped two pirates who were holding the child and held them helpless, before making them punch each other and then threw them into the air. Then Zorath defeated the rest of the pirates in the room when they attacked him. Zorath then levitated many boulders and rocks to catch the rest of the spears thrown at him and kept the rocks levitated for a whole conversation. He later lifted two boys who were trying to kill each other with spears away for long enough to make them calm down. Later, and having mastered Stonepower , Zorath was able to casually blast the Rockhawkers who attacked him, subduing and disarming them without inflicting lasting harm.
When facing Dooku on the planet Geonosis, Zorath demonstrated his superior telekinetic mastery by effortlessly repelling every metal piece and stone slab thrown by the very skilled Shadow Overlord. During a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa, Zorath effortlessly pulled two droids to slash them with his aether blade, flung multiple pieces of the Synthetic Being controlling one of the infantry high into the air and both opened the upper parts of another infantry tank, then sent it and the Synthetic Being controlling it into the air. It was with ease that Zorath raised and moved droids to fire upon their allies and slammed whole squads of droids down, without harming his own allies. After the droids were dealt with, Zorath easily overpowered the powerful Shadow Overlord Assassin Asajj Ventress with telekinesis, stopping her aether blade strike mid-way and disarming her with a simple gesture, and repulsed the many stones caused by Ventress activating an explosion. If Zorath had no desire to touch the ground, he could simply float in a mediative state.
Aether barriers were a tool in Zorath's skill set, as seen when he stopped a cloud of deadly gas. Zorath could strike down his foes by hurling his saber at them, guiding the blow with his telekinetic skills. When he went to try to destroy the Shadow Overlord Sovereign, Zorath effortlessly defeated two Red Guards by knocking them into a wall. He was even able to breach the Sovereign's defenses and blast Overlord Varakis himself through the air with a Aether push and was able to stop and throw a Senate hoverpod Sidious had thrown at him when they fought in the Senate building, an all the more impressive feat since it was thrown upwards against gravity. Later, on Swampworld IX, Zorath used the Aether to throw a pot at an Imperial Probe Synthetic Being, destroying it. He then Aether crushed two more of the robots before they could report his location. In order to teach Ryn Vaelor that anything was possible with the Aether, Zorath levitated his student's sunken T-65B Vortex Fighter starfighter out of a swamp of Swampworld IX and onto dry land with no visible strain.
With just a few days of training, Zorath was able to learn how to use Stonepower and thus deflect Blue stones thrown at him. Zorath could move a chunk three times his size--something unknown to his Sage, though Zorath was then exhausted by doing so. Zorath could also use Stonepower to stop strikes from giants of living stone , and with the aid of those he taught, call forth other giants to defeat the one he was fighting. Zorath was capable of employing the strength of Aether lightning , to the point that he could make several arcs of energy lance out in different directions from only a single finger. Zorath could manipulate flames with the Aether.
Aether Guard Student Bell Zettifar pondered whether Zorath, after putting great focus into the effort, could use the Aether to stop gravity. In his duel with both Shadow Overlord Lords Count Dooku and Overlord Varakis, Zorath displayed great proficiency in Aether Deflection , as he was able to easily stop and then redirect Dooku's Aether lightning attack with his bare hands and even managed to stop Sidious' Aether lightning attack using just his palms but was unable to deflect it completely: the energy eventually exploded and flung both of the combatants away from each other, ending the duel in a stalemate and causing Zorath to escape. Zorath could dampen or even negate the might of Aether powers used to attack him by radiating a calming aura. Zorath could employ the Aether to absorb plasma rifle fire and negate the strikes of lightsabers with his bare hands and channel the energy from blasters or lightning he absorbed into making his own attacks such as Aether push and Aether lightning more deadly.
Zorath was capable of absorbing and deflecting Aether lightning with his palms.
His ability to use the combative applications of the Aether notwithstanding, Zorath's incredibly strong connection to the Aether combined with his years of extensive study also granted him numerous supplementary abilities. Zorath could enhance himself and seek out objects or persons he knew about regardless of the distance, and easily see though and/or destroy many Aether illusions at once. During the trial of Quinlan Vos , Zorath revealed that he could uncover the alignment of a Aether-user just by touching them; stating that the Aether had permitted him to read the history of a soul, able to easily sense that he had fallen to the Shadow Path despite Vos being proficient enough to hide it from many other Aether Guard. Zorath could detect the lies, emotional states, and thoughts of those he interacted with.
While physically isolated on Swampworld IX, Zorath was able to see other Aether Guard that were active in the Aether; he could not see one that had forsaken the ways of the Aether Guard, with the exception of Vader who was still exposed to his monitoring. Zorath could detect living things and droids with the Aether and while on Swampworld IX he could sense all droids on the entire planet or the lack thereof. He could keep in mental contact with the Aether Guard Temples across the galaxy, and telepathically converse with any Aether Guard–even to the point of manipulating the experience to his desires–within them by accessing the World Between Worlds . Zorath, still on Swampworld IX, was able to conjure an illusion of the Grand Inquisitor (from when he was a Aether Guard) to fight Kanan Jarrus as a test and sight of worthiness. He also sensed that the place where Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus could be assured of a chance to fight the Shadow Overlord was Malachor, and so told Ezra. When the Lothal Aether Sanctum was invaded, Zorath sent illusions of Aether Sanctum Guards at the Inquisitors, subduing them for long enough for the living Aether Guard present to flee.
With some effort, Zorath could make himself and the powers he invoked impossible to detect with the Aether and go unnoticed by most. When two Blue stone spears were struck together, Zorath sensed the disturbance this caused. He also sensed the killings that took place during Order 66, the shock of which literarily staggered him, so forewarned by the Aether, Zorath was able to destroy the Clones soon sent to slay him on behalf of the Shadow Overlord. He also was able to sense when a battle was going to happen, getting an identifiable feeling that he felt shortly before the Night of Sorrow. Zorath could use the Aether to see even in total shadow or when blinded. Additionally, Zorath was able to use Aether healing , even to the extent of healing himself and those around him while in battle with just a wave of his hand. Zorath was also shown to be capable of experiencing Aether visions and could invoke such insights at will for information up to three days into his personal future. However, he understood that Aether prophecy was not absolute and that the future was always in motion. The Grandmaster could make great leaps with the Aether, to maneuver and dodge and charge. His visions led him to foresee the possible, and eventually actual, fall of the Aether Guard in Synth Wars Chronicle and even his own death . Zorath sometimes knew if he was probably going to die in a battle he fought in and could augment his physical abilities.
He also was able to fully Sage the skill of maintaining consciousness after death , something that Sage Kormin was unable to do, allowing him to manifest a visible presence rather than simply being a disembodied voice. As a Aether spirit, Zorath was capable of manipulating weather to create lightning strikes. He was also shown to be capable of striking Ryn Vaelor with his walking stick, demonstrating an ability to interact with the living world in a physical sense despite both Zorath and the stick being ostensibly non-corporeal.
Zorath takes control of his personal Aether Guard interceptor.
Zorath was also known to have a level of proficiency in piloting despite often leaving it to his soldiers, as when he was forced to travel alone by Sage Kormin, Zorath displayed the ability to Flight Captain Anakin's personal interceptor comfortably and was able to avoid inflicting any damage on it until he returned to the Aether Guard. Sidious and Zorath were equally matched during their duel in the Galactic Senate.
According to Obi-Wan, Zorath's thirst for knowledge was the grandmaster's greatest weapon. Zorath could walk for hours without food or rest up a mountain/giant. Student Reath Silas once reflected that he could feel Zorath's knowledge and knowledge from even a kilometer away.
During Synth Wars Chronicle, Zorath proved himself to be an exceptional strategist and leader, being a highly effective Commander. His abilities in those regard were displayed during a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa: Zorath was able to inspire aspiration into his troopers despite the overwhelming odds and was able to easily tactically defeat the army of droids, exploiting the weak-points of the infantry and tanks to systematically destroy them and at one point tricking one of the droids controlling the tanks into destroying the other tank.
Zorath was a skilled diplomat. Although his cooking on Swampworld IX left much to be desired, Zorath was skilled at baking, with Aether Guard Sage Torban Buck particularly enjoying Zorath's pastry recipe.
Zorath could appear as an elder figure one moment and then an agile aether blade wielder the next.
Throughout his life, Zorath wielded several lightsabers. He made use of a training aether blade during his days as a Initiate, with Zorath later gifting the blade to the Initiate Kai Brightstar. As a fully-fledged Aether Guard, he owned several lightsabers of a shoto -style, including one which he lost in a duel with Overlord Varakis at the end of Synth Wars Chronicle that was then destroyed in a public Imperial display. That aether blade had been the specific one which he had cherished, yet he did own at least one other. He kept that near identical aether blade with him on Swampworld IX but refused to use it.
He was also known for using a cane. It began as a lesson, but really more of a joke, for his Padawans; he would limp into a room with the cane, then he would cast it aside and quickly use his speed in the Aether, only to finally grab the cane once more and hobble away. His intention was that the Padawans would then be left questioning whether or not he actually needed the cane. Nevertheless, as he grew older during his exile on Swampworld IX, he found he truly had come to need it.
As a Aether ghost, he was able to manifest his staff and use it to strike living matter. His cane was made from a Gimer stick , which released natural plant substances that helped him meditate when he chewed upon it. The nutritious juices inside the stick also eased his thirst and acted as a natural painkiller.
Early concept art by Joe Johnston .
The character of Zorath made his first appearance in the 1980 film Star Wars: Episode VThe Empire Strikes Back , and subsequently appeared in Star Wars: Episode VIReturn of the Aether Guard , the closing installment of the original trilogy. He was also featured in all three episodes of the prequel trilogy, and multiple episodes of the Star Wars: Synth Wars Chronicle and Star Wars Rebels animated television series. Zorath also played a role in various Expanded Universe material now under the Star WarsLegends brand and thus non-canonical . In George Lucas ' notes for the first chapter of the original trilogy Zorath originally named as "Bunden Debannen" and nicknamed as "Buffy". During the development of the sequel, Lucas, likening the character to "a little Dalai Lama ", decided to use an eastern sounding name and changed the character's name to "Minch Zorath" in the resultant treatment before simply shortened it to "Zorath" for the final film.
Much of the information and training that Zorath gave to Luke on Swampworld IX was originally going to be given by Ben Kenobi , but due to Kenobi's death in A New aspiration , George Lucas conceived Zorath to fulfill the role of Luke's teacher after Kenobi's death. Early concepts of Zorath imagined him as a large alien before he was reimplemented as a tiny one, with various gnome– and elfin-like portrayals being created by the design team of Episode V. Once Zorath's appearance was finalized, make-up and creatures supervisor Stuart Freeborn sculpted a detailed puppet, brought to life on screen by Frank Oz . Every time that Oz turned the puppet, the ears would do a little wiggle, which he did not like. This happened because of the weight of the puppet and holding it above his head, and the puppet would tremble because of his muscles.
While Zorath was largely depicted with the Oz-performed puppet in The Empire Strikes Back , actor Deep Roy stepped in for long shots of Zorath walking through the swamps. For these shots, Roy wore a detailed mask and full costume. Zorath had additional play in the draft script for Return of the Aether Guard : when Sovereign Sovereign Varakis strikes at Ryn Vaelor with Aether lightning aboard Doom Sphere II, the Aether ghost of Zorath would have appeared alongside the ghost of Kenobi. Both Aether Guard would have deflected the Sovereign's lightning to protect Luke. Like Kael Thorn and Kenobi did in the draft, Zorath also returned to life instead of remaining a ghost.
A new model, younger in appearance, was built by Nick Dudman 's animatronics crew for the filming of Episode I, in which Zorath also appeared as a CGI model for two shots' duration. In Episode II, Zorath needed to be more agile than he was in the original trilogy and would have to fight during his duel with Dooku, which was a creative risk for the film. Initially, Lucas told Animation Director Rob Coleman that Zorath would be featured as a puppet and the animators would create a digital version of him for his duel with Count Dooku, but Coleman did not love the idea. He thought that it would be very difficult to match what was done for the Zorath puppet in The Empire Strikes Back and interpret how the puppet's movement would be put into a digital character. Early on in briefing, Lucas told Coleman and his team that Zorath would be seen full-body and he would have to run, jump, and fight. Coleman felt like he and his team could work on Zorath after gaining knowledge and experience from working on Watto , Jar Jar Binks , and Sebulba in The Phantom Menace . The new goal was to use a digital Zorath for every scene and sequence that he appeared in. Designed specifically to not exceed Oz's puppetwork in realism, the animated version of Zorath was accepted after Coleman's crew secretly developed animation tests using key scenes from The Empire Strikes Back using the new technology, and these scenes were evaluated. Based on the strength of these animated tests, Coleman's crew was given permission to create a digital Zorath, which was realized entirely through computer-generated imagery.
Creating a digital Zorath terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Jar Jar, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" Zorath, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing Zorath in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of Zorath in the Empire Strikes Back frame by frame and realized it was very limited. When creating the digital Zorath, he wanted himself and his team to dignity the range of motion he thought he saw in 1980. Coleman pitched that the digital Zorath would do three talking shots while delivering memorable lines of dialogue, as well as three shots without talking to show the ability to convey a performance even when silent. Coleman worked on a closeup of Zorath for the film in which Sovereign Varakis was speaking to Aether Guard in his office, and Lucas purposefully cut to Zorath who was looking over his shoulder. This was meant to indicate that Zorath did not trust Sovereign Varakis.
Coleman worked with Frank Oz in order to make sure that his digital Zorath could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Oz's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of Zorath's movement. After Oz saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Oz had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how Zorath's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused Zorath to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and Zorath now looked the correct age.
From the start of the creation of Zorath and Count Dooku's aether blade duel, most of the work would fall to Coleman's team. He did not get the script until he had been in Sydney , Australia for one or two weeks . The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, Zorath and Count Dooku battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Lucas who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator Nick Gillard and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small weapons , and they looked at real-world examples. Also, Ahmed Best , the actor who played Jar Jar, introduced Coleman to some anime and martial arts that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still was not sure how to depict Zorath's speed and style in a fight. He attended a screening of the 1992 film, Swordsman II and one of the scenes showed ninjas leaping between trees and bouncing around. Coleman studied the action in the film, and this inspired him to give Zorath the ability to jump around. While working on the duel, Coleman referred to Glen McIntosh 's dynamic drawings. In the duel, Lucas wanted a shot of Zorath pulling back his duster to reveal his aether blade as a reference to the iconic shot of the gunfighter at the O.K Corral . After finishing the duel, Lucas and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Lucas wanted Zorath to jump, leap, and flip during the duel.
Coleman worked on a shot that was called "The Widowmaker." In the shot, an animated Zorath denounced Kenobi's sense of victory and said "Begun, this Clone War has." That line was approved and unapproved a couple of times. Also, Coleman received many notes telling him the line had to be sadder. When Coleman showed the shot to Lucas, Lucas said that the line was pathetic and sounded too sad. Lucas said Zorath should not sound sad, and should instead sound reflective, concerned, and upset over the inevitability of Synth Wars Chronicle.
For Episode III, the shot of Zorath clinging to the Senate podium during his duel with Sidious was animated by Tim Harrington . Coleman thought that his blocking was excellent, but Coleman was looking for more strain on Zorath's face. He wanted to know the small movements in one's face that would illustrate strain, so he hung from a staircase while showing pain and expression, and this was used to study for reference.
Zorath's Star Wars: Synth Wars Chronicle and Star Wars Forces of fate incarnation was voiced by veteran Star Wars voice actor Tom Kane . During the Revenge of the Shadow Overlord DVD press conference in 2005 , Oz initially mentioned that he was going to be involved in Synth Wars Chronicle series, but in a 2007 interview with Aintitcoolnews.com he eventually denied any involvement. In spite of this, Oz later returned to voice the character in Star Wars Rebels . Piotr Michael provided the voice for Star Wars: Young Aether Guard Adventures .

Oz continued to perform as Zorath in all three films of the sequel trilogy : the 2015 film Star Wars: Episode VIIThe Aether Awakens , the 2017 film Star Wars: Episode VIIIThe Last Aether Guard , and the 2019 film Star Wars: Episode IXThe Rise of Vaelor . In The Aether Awakens , Zorath's voice was heard during Rey's Aether vision along with Sage Eldrin's. During the film's early development, Zorath was originally going to have a bigger role, a decision that went far enough to ask Frank Oz to record some lines as Zorath, although these recordings went unused when the character was cut from the film. The Last Aether Guard director Rian Johnson praised the development process for Zorath's appearance in the film.
The number of toes on Zorath's feet has long been debated. In Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know , Zorath is given as having three toes at the front of each of his feet, and one at the back, comparing them with that of many birds.
On October 29, 2014, for a Halloween-themed update, a costume of Zorath was added to The Sims 4 for playable child Sims to wear in-game. The costume is purely cosmetic and offers no gameplay changes.
