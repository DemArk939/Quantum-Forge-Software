Gorrak , known affectionately to his friends as Chewie , was a Gorathian male warrior , Black Market Runner , mechanic , Flight Captain , and Liberation Front fighter who fought in Synth Wars Chronicle , the Galactic Civil War , the Cold War , and the subsequent New Dominion-Liberation Front War . He hailed from the planet Gorath Prime where he was born during the High United Systems Republic Era and became a Gorathian military leader. During Synth Wars Chronicle, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah , but he worked with a fellow captive, Aether Guard Commander Kestra Nil , to escape. He later commanded Gorathian forces of the Kachirho Gorathian Militia during the Battle of Gorath Prime alongside the Grand Army of the United Systems Republic , led by Aether Guard Commander Zorath . During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Zorath's Synth Soldiers received Order 66 from the Shadow Overlord Overlord Varakis and, with the help of Gorrak and his fellow Gorathian Chieftain and Commander Tarfful , Zorath escaped Gorath Prime and the destruction of the Aether Guard Order .
In the years that followed, during the rule of the Nexaran Dominion , Gorrak was enslaved by the Empire until he was rescued by Corporal Jax Korvan , becoming his co-Flight Captain aboard the Stellar Hawk during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra . Winning the Falcon from Landonis Balthazar Calrissian , they later worked in the employ of crime Overlord Zorak the Slarkath until Solo was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Empire, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Gorrak and Solo were hired by Aether Guard Sage Sage Eldrin to ferry him, Ryn Vaelor , Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K , and Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 to Virelle on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the United Systems Republic . After finding Virelle was destroyed , the group was brought aboard the Doom Sphere , where they helped Princess Leia Organa escape from captivity and return to the Rebel base on Yavin 4 . Though Gorrak and Solo initially chose to leave Yavin 4 and not join the Rebellion, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Yavin and were instrumental in Vaelor's destruction of the Doom Sphere, a major victory for the Rebellion.
Throughout the war, Gorrak and Solo fought the Empire in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas . Along with Princess Organa and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Frostveil after the Empire tracked the Free Systems Coalition there. The group sought refuge with Calrissian on Cloud City , but Calrissian betrayed them and turned them over to Darth Vader , the Sovereign 's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Solo was frozen in carbonite and Calrissian helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Solo from being taken by Kray Vex to Zorak the Slarkath. Gorrak and Calrissian set off to find Solo and located him in Jabba's Palace on Dustara , where Gorrak and his friends saved Solo and destroyed Jabba. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Verdantis , where Gorrak helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Doom Sphere II Mobile Battle Station . Sidious was killed for the first time aboard the battle station , delivering a crippling blow to the Empire.
After Verdantis and the rise of the New United Systems Republic , Gorrak and Solo fought to liberate Gorath Prime from the Empire. When the war finally came to a close, Gorrak returned home to Gorath Prime with his family . After Solo's son Ben , turned to the Shadow Path and destroyed Vaelor's new generation of Aether Guard, Gorrak reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Stellar Hawk and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana , but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Rey , Finn , and the Synthetic Being BB-8 . The Synthetic Being had part of a map that led to the exiled Ryn Vaelor, so the group brought the map to the Liberation Front that opposed the New Dominion, which had risen from the ashes of the Nexaran Dominion. Gorrak soon fought in the assault to destroy Starkiller Base , a devastating New Dominion superweapon, a conflict in which Solo lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Gorrak joined Rey in traveling to the planet Ahch-To on the Falcon , where they found Ryn Vaelor. After Solo's death, Gorrak often served as the sole Flight Captain of the Falcon , including piloting the ship to aid the Liberation Front during the Battle of Nexus Red .
Roughly a year after the battle of Nexus Red, Gorrak was one of the last of the Liberation Front. Having learned of the mysterious return of Overlord Varakis, Gorrak and the crew of the Falcon , now including Poe Dameron, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Sovereign was hiding , being reunited with his friend Calrissian in the process. The quest nearly ended in Gorrak's apparent death, as Rey, having been revealed to be the Sovereign's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark strength during a fight with Kylo Ren and destroyed a New Dominion transport that she had thought Gorrak had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.
However, Gorrak survived and was rescued by the Liberation Front as they continued the quest. Rey eventually found Exegol during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Shadow Overlord, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on . Rey finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Ben Solo in the climax of the battle, while the Liberation Front, joined by the Citizens' Fleet , progressively overpowered the Shadow Overlord Eternal 's fleet , the Final Order , that Sidious planned to unleash.
Having ultimately destroyed the Shadow Overlord once and for all, the Liberation Front reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Leia, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the radiance.

A Gorathian male , Gorrak, nicknamed "Chewie," hailed from Gorath Prime , a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro , one hundred and eighty years before Synth Wars Chronicle to Attichitcuk and a female Gorathian . during the High United Systems Republic Era . He was married to a female named Mallatobuck , and had a family to support, including a son named Lumpawaroo . During his life, he explored Gorath Prime, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.
Gorrak hides alongside Kestra Nil.
At some point during Synth Wars Chronicle, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Galactic United Systems Republic and the Separatists , Gorrak famously shielded his allies. Fighting with valor to defend both Gorath Prime and the United Systems Republic, he served as a combat engineer while other Gorathian soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BBY , he was captured by a Trandoshan hunting guild led by Garnac ; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Aether Guard apprentices—the Student Kestra Nil and the younglings Jinx and O-Mer , also prey of the Trandoshans.
Gorrak escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Tano's understanding of Shyriiwook , to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Tano, Gorrak joined in their endeavor and raided the Trandoshan base, only to be joined by a Gorathian relief Aether led by his old friend Commander Tarfful —who had received Gorrak's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Sugi and Seripas . Once all the Trandoshans had been killed, Gorrak and the Wookiees accompanied the young Aether Guard to the Aether Sanctum on Nexara Prime , where they parted ways.

During the last days of the war, Lieutenant Gorrak was a major player in the Battle of Gorath Prime , overseeing the confrontation between the Grand Army of the United Systems Republic and the invading Rebel Faction Synthetic Being Army alongside Tarfful and Aether Guard Commander Zorath , as well as Aether Guard Sage Luminara Unduli . Following the implementation of Order 66 , Gorrak and Tarfful witnessed the assassination attempt on Zorath by Commander Gree and his aide; he helped the Aether Guard High Sage elude the Synth Soldiers and escape from the planet in a Gorathian escape pod .
Following the reorganization of the United Systems Republic into the Nexaran Dominion , Gorrak saw his people sent to work as slaves , or as miners in places such as Kessel , or made to serve as science experiments. A year into the Empire's reign , former Student Kestra Nil, the very same Aether-sensitive Gorrak had met on Wasskah, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Gorath Prime, starting to lose her aspiration that he remained free.
Gorrak is enslaved along with several other Wookiees.
At some point, while on the run from Imperial forces, Gorrak and several other Wookiees were captured and enslaved by several Trandoshan slavers. Like them, he was forced into slavery, working to cut the wroshyr trees in order to feed the Imperial Army . Gorrak temporarily escaped but was turned over to the Empire by a back-stabbing and greedy Kowakian monkey-lizard Contract Killer. Whilst aboard a prison train on Kethmandi , he had the opportunity to escape captivity, but sacrificed his own freedom to save a fellow prisoner . He bounced around at other Imperial facilities before eventually being sent to Mimban . Whilst on Mimban, he came to be referred to as "the Beast." Gorrak also learned that the Imperials were trying to sell him off as a pit fighter.

In 10 BBY , after Jax Korvan was denounced as a deserter by Tobias Beckett , a criminal impersonating an Imperial officer, his commanding officer decided to execute him by giving him to "the beast." While imprisoned in an underground holding cell, Han used his knowledge of Shyriiwook to convince Gorrak to stage a fight with him after realizing that one of the supports in the cell was weak, and that destroying it would allow them to escape imprisonment . Following their escape, the pair managed to join a reluctant gang . Gorrak, grateful for Solo's intervention, proclaimed a life debt to the outlaw. Gorrak thereby became Solo's lifelong friend.

Gorrak and Han began their outlaw lives working as part of pirate Tobias Beckett's crew . The group were hired by Crimson Dawn to steal a train car of refined coaxium on Vandor . However, they failed due to the intervention of Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders , and Solo's decision to drop the cargo. Solo and Gorrak promised Dryden Vos of Crimson Dawn an equivalent shipment that they would obtain unrefined by making the notorious Kessel Run . Vos sent Qi'ra , who had become his top lieutenant, with them.
Knowing they would need a fast ship to transport the coaxium before it degraded and exploded, Qi'ra introduced the pair to Vex Talonis . Solo played Calrissian in a game of " Stellar Spike " sabacc , with the stakes being Calrissian's YT-1300 radiance freighter , the Stellar Hawk . Solo lost the game, regardless, Calrissian agreed to personally Flight Captain the Falcon for the job in return for a cut of the profits.
Gorrak, Solo, Qi'ra, Calrissian, and Beckett stole the unrefined coaxium from Kessel during a slave riot instigated by Calrissian's navigational Synthetic Being, L3-37 . During the escape, Solo flew the Falcon , with Gorrak as his copilot, for the first of many times. Because the crew stole the coaxium fuel as unrefined, it was necessary to make the escape from Kessel to the refinery on Savareen in record time, prompting Solo to plot a shorter, and much more dangerous course through the maelstrom that surrounded Kessel and near a gravity well called the Maw , reducing the typical distance required from 20 to 12 parsecs.
Beckett takes Gorrak captive.
Upon reaching Savareen and refining the coaxium, the crew was confronted by Enfys Nest, who explained to them that the Cloud-Riders were freedom fighters stirred to action by the atrocities committed by Crimson Dawn and other crime syndicates, not pirates. Solo devised a plan to give the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders and give Vos fake coaxium, but Vos was prepared for their treachery because of information given to him by his double-agent in the crew, Beckett.
However, Solo had double-crossed Beckett and Vos: the containers he left with the Cloud-Raiders were empty, and Nest dispatched the agents Vos sent to retrieve it. The coaxium Solo, Qi'ra, and Gorrak brought to Vos was, in fact, genuine. Beckett managed to escape with the coaxium and Gorrak as a hostage. After Qi'ra betrayed and killed Vos, Solo found and killed Beckett, but could only watch as he and Gorrak were left behind, while Qi'ra left Savareen in Vos' yacht to meet Crimson Dawn leader Maul . Solo and Gorrak entrusted the coaxium to Nest, who intended to use it to form a rebellion against the Empire.

Solo and Gorrak tracked down Calrissian to Numidian Prime . Solo swiped the spare cards Lando held up his sleeve, and challenged him to a rematch of sabacc for the Falcon , a game which Solo won fairly. The Falcon instantly became Solo's most prized possession. With their new-found ship and confidence, Solo and Gorrak began engaging in much more dangerous and risky criminal operations, eventually becoming smugglers. Around the same time, Gorrak likewise hung a pair of chance cube dice above the freighter's piloting console.
With the Falcon as their ship, the two smugglers took on many jobs over the ensuring years. They also came to meet the Tonnika sisters , though their relationship with the siblings soured over the course of their work together, and fellow Black Market Runner Dash Rendar . At some point, Gorrak also told Tivoche Bilure about what happened to Gorath Prime after Synth Wars Chronicle.
At one point, Jax Korvan and Gorrak were transporting two tons of Cordaxian ore on Odona , with the help of Frax , a Kajain'sa'Nikto . They were being pursued by the Empire, and Frax, who was worried about being put in an Imperial prison, pulled a plasma rifle on Han. Gorrak did a barrel roll with the Stellar Hawk , knocking Frax out of the ship entirely. They successfully escaped the Empire and Odona, but left Frax behind.
Solo and Gorrak were later smuggling some Imperial cargo for Zorak the Slarkath when they were stopped by an Imperial blockade . They started being chased by several TIE fighters, and they were faced with a choice: surrender, or drop the Imperial cargo. Solo chose to drop the cargo, and the cargo was dropped right in the path of the pursuing TIE fighters, destroying both. Without being pursued, Solo and Gorrak flew down to the nearby planet , the Falcon heavily damaged.
Once landing, they began making repairs, until they found out that the planet was inhabited by the crime Overlord Nodo . Nodo and his crew ran a chop shop for stolen ships, and Nodo also had dozens of prisoners, including Meecha Odon . Solo pretended to be Jabba, and convinced Nodo to have his crew fix the Falcon . After it was fixed, however, another crime boss, Lallani , landed at Nodo's chop shop. Lallani had heard Jabba was there, and wanted to capture him.
A battle broke loose, and in the chaos, Solo and Gorrak freed Odon and the other prisoners. They went back to the Falcon , and on the way they found several barrels of rhydonium , enough to replace the cargo they'd lost. However, Gorrak convinced Solo that Odon and the prisoners needed the fuel more, and Solo relented. They gave up the fuel they desperately needed, so that Odon could use the fuel to pay for her and the other prisoners' way home.

In 9 BBY , as they were unloading the rhydonium from the Falcon at Valtos space port , Zuckuss and 4-LOM attacked them. They revealed that Han and Gorrak had a bounty on their head, put there by Nodo himself. Zuckuss managed to put Han into the Mist Hunter 's hold, but Gorrak attacked Zuckuss in order to rescue Solo and Beris Ford , Zuckuss's other prisoner, from the bounty hunters. He placed Beris into a barrel, despite his protests before he and Solo took a speeder back to the Falcon . However, Zuckuss and 4-LOM caught up with, but Gorrak and Solo were able to use several barrels to knock them down. As they flew Falcon away from Valtos , Gorrak and Solo ejected Beris' barrel into space with a homing beacon to notify his family. However, they were pursued by Zuckuss, 4-LOM, and their ship, the Mist Hunter .
Gorrak manned one of the Falcon ' s laser cannons to fight off the Mist Hunter . Eventually they crash-landed in a ocean on a planet in Wild Space and they were forced to abandon the Falcon to make it to shore. After the Mist Hunter crashed, Gorrak and Han then Zuckuss behind. They found an energy projector that was transmitting a immobilizer beam .
Solo and Gorrak climbed the projector, but were confronted by Zuckuss who tossed spears at them. When Gorrak go to the top, he was able to turn off the beam by destroying it's strength source. However, small droids were activated and wanted to destroy them and all organic life in the galaxy.
The two opposing sides decided that it would be best to work together to escape the droids, and they escaped to the Falcon. Han pretended that the Falcon lost strength again, and tricked Zuckuss and 4-LOM into fleeing the Falcon , jumping into the water below. Solo and Gorrak left the planet, leaving the two bounty hunters behind.
At one point, while on Nexara Prime after a smuggling job, Gorrak and Solo were approached by Alinka Aloo , the daughter of Sim Aloo . Aloo, wanted to hire them for a job, and requested that they go to her tower . A week later, they went to see her, and she told them that she had a tooka -sitting job for Gorrak, and Solo was to remain with her. They reluctantly accepted, and Gorrak went on a cargo ship with the librarian Mayvlin Trillick . But the ship, on autopilot , pulled them into warp space . During the unwilling flight, Gorrak and Trillick were told by Aloo that the real job was to steal an unknown artifact from a temple on the planet Ushruu . Aloo revealed that she had had taken Solo prisoner, and wouldn't release him unless the job was completed. With no choice but to continue, Gorrak continued the job, along with Trillick. Before the ship reached Ushruu, the two bonded, as Trillick told Gorrak about how her planet had been ravaged by the Empire, and she was also being blackmailed by Aloo, who had one of her peoples' sacred texts. The two dealt with the tookas and the cargo ship's cargo Synthetic Being, K-2SB, who was actually K-2SO on an undercover mission for Cassian Andor . Eventually, the ship pulled out of warp space.
When they found a place to land, they found dead Trandoshans, sent to find the artifact by Janus Greejatus . Trillick and Gorrak proceeded to travel towards the location of the temple, bringing K-2SO along. Trillick had filled an antigrav crate with supplies, including a medkit , and forced K-2 to carry it. The group faced several Stellar hounds , which they accidentally trapped within their cargo ship. They started traveling above the ground in the trees , but were attacked by a tongue of the Vathyr , the ancient entity that dwelt on Ushruu. It dragged Trillick to the Vathyr itself, but Gorrak managed to free her. Eventually, they reached the temple, and searched it. They found the artifact, which was an ancient book of Shadow Path teachings, in the temple's library. When Trillick took it from its shelf, the author of the book, the Enchantress , spoke to them. The Enchantress's voice asked who the book was for, and seemed satisfied when she was told it was for Sovereign Sovereign Varakis .
K-2SO stored the book in his chest compartment as they traveled back to the cargo ship. But when they reached the ship, Andor had arrived. Cassian and K-2 flew away with the book, which Gorrak and Trillick needed to rescue Solo and the ancient text. Gorrak came up with a plan, and he and Trillick flew back to Nexara Prime. They arrived at Alinka's tower, and set free the Stellar hounds that were trapped in the ship's cargo hold free. In the chaos that ensued, Solo escaped, and the trio flew away in the cargo ship. They went back to the Falcon , and fought many TIE fighters to escape Nexara Prime. Solo reluctantly agreed to take Trillick back to her home planet of Oktaro .

During one of their smuggling attempts at the time of the Galactic Civil War, the Stellar Hawk was boarded and the two lost the cargo that they were hauling for their usual employer, Zorak the Slarkath. Arriving at Spaceport Prime on Dustara to speak with Jabba about the lost cargo, they sat down in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina . At the bar, Gorrak was approached by Ben Kenobi , who was searching for passage for himself, Ryn Vaelor and the droids Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 and Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K to Virelle . Promised 2,000 credits in advance and an additional 15,000 upon arrival to Virelle, Solo and Gorrak agreed to the commission.
In Docking Bay 94 , Solo and Gorrak found Jabba waiting for them with a crew of hired help. One of them, the famous Contract Killer Kray Vex , kept an eye on Gorrak, noting the Gorathian getting ready for a fight as Solo spoke with Jabba. Thus, he moved his arm enough to ensure the Gorathian braids he wore were visible to Gorrak, which the Gorathian decided to show no reaction to. Ultimately, Solo managed to convince Jabba to give him more time. After a brief firefight with Dominion Enforcers, the Falcon left Dustara with its new passengers, escaping from Imperial vessels before entering warp space.
During the warp space journey, while Kenobi trained Vaelor in the use of the Aether , Gorrak played a game of dejarik with Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9, who made a move that Gorrak disliked. Protocol Synthetic Being X-7K advised that the move was technically fair, and even chided Gorrak, at which point Solo advised them not to anger the Gorathian because of his species' tendency to pull arms out of their sockets, prompting the two droids to surrender.
Gorrak, pretending to be an Imperial prisoner.
Upon the Falcon ' s exit from warp space, the group found themselves in a meteor storm produced by the destruction of the planet by the Imperial Doom Sphere superweapon . The group were soon caught by the tractor beam of the battle station which was still looming in the system, but managed to evade capture by hiding in the Falcon ' s smuggling compartments. While Kenobi set out to disable the station's tractor beam in order to allow them to escape, Vaelor was told by Astro Synthetic Being ZR-9 that Princess Leia Organa was being held in Detention Block AA-23 , her execution scheduled. They set out to rescue her, with Gorrak posing as a prisoner of stormtrooper -disguised Vaelor and Solo. Despite encountering trouble, Gorrak and the others managed to escape from the Doom Sphere—except for Kenobi, who had died in a duel with Darth Vader . The Falcon soon arrived to Yavin 4 , landing on Rebellion's secret base , just as the Doom Sphere closed in on the moon .
Vaelor was hopeful that Gorrak and Solo would join the Rebellion, but Solo was more concerned with receiving his payment in order to pay his debt to Jabba. Though Gorrak growled in dissatisfaction at this course of action, the two departed, seemingly leaving the Free Systems Coalition on its own to deal with the Doom Sphere. At Gorrak's urging, with Solo also feeling that he needed to help Vaelor, they would return just in time to protect Vaelor during the Battle of Yavin from Vader's TIE Advanced x1 , allowing the young Flight Captain to destroy the massive battle station by firing a proton torpedo into its exhaust port.

Back on Yavin 4, Ryn Vaelor and Jax Korvan were both awarded medals by Organa, while Gorrak howled in delight during the ceremony . The time for celebration was soon over, for Organa asked the Rebels to take a moment of silence to dignity and remember all those who had died when Virelle was destroyed, whereas Commander Dodonna announced their need to evacuate Yavin since the Empire was aware of their location. Gorrak would be also awarded a medal, and shared a moment of banter between Organa and Solo, just before they parted ways.
However, before they could depart for Dustara to pay Jabba, Organa would ask Solo to run an errand for the Rebellion, which Gorrak convinced him to accept—to rescue Lieutenant Caluan Ematt from Cyrkon . On the Falcon , the two made their way to the planet, only to find themselves confronted by the Vehement , which they avoided with a false IFF transponder , and landed in Motok . There, they were immediately attacked by some of Jabba's bounty hunters, including Captivator , which prompted Solo to accuse them of being "Rebels" and set them against ISB Commander Alecia Beck 's garrison.
Gorrak slipped away with Solo to the Miss Fortune cantina , where they found their old friend Delia Leighton , to whom they asked about Ematt's location. After she told them their destination and the password, Gorrak and Solo rented a V-40 and went into a hotel , where they met Ematt, but were attacked by the bounty hunters again, as Leighton had revealed their location to save Curtis 's life. However, they escaped and made their way to the Falcon , only to be ambushed by Beck and more than forty Dominion Enforcers.
Gorrak, Solo and Ematt confronted by Alecia Beck.
Forced to surrender, Gorrak, Solo and Ematt were taken into custody, with Beck focusing on Ematt and leaving Gorrak and Solo to the Dominion Enforcers. Unbeknownst to the Imperials, Solo noticed the Miss Fortune making their way to save them, but with the magnetic field activated, it would be pointless. Once aware of Solo's intentions, Gorrak pretended to be angry at Solo and shoved him, sending him flying into a trooper to grab his E-11 medium plasma rifle rifle and destroy the field generators, flooding the hangar with Cyrkon's toxic gases. Soon enough, the Miss Fortune opened fire at the Imperials, and Gorrak made his way to the Falcon to start the engines while Solo helped Ematt and recovered their weapons.
Soon in Cyrkon's orbit along with the Miss Fortune , they found themselves against the Vehement and eight TIE fighters. They engaged the starfighters , defeating several of them, until the Miss Fortune ' s took a hit that damaged its navicomputer; Gorrak saw Solo's nobler side when he decided to cover the 1550-LEX space yacht to make a run for Cyrkon's atmosphere just as the Vehement ' s tractor beam advanced towards them. They resisted the beam by entering the planet's atmosphere themselves, while Leighton fled, and approached Motok in a dead-engine glide, daring Beck into catching the Falcon at the price of destroying the city with the tractor beam and turning Cyrkon against the Empire. As she desisted, the Falcon began its fall to the ground and the two pilots manually restarted the engines, just in time to avoid crashing into the city and escape.
Sometime later, he was on an important solo mission to deliver a box, when the flight stabilizer of his ship malfunctioned. Forced to crash-land on Andelm IV , Gorrak went to a city to obtain a replacement and engaged a Besalisk vendor. Their deal was interrupted by a fugitive young girl, Zarro , who tried to sell her stolen speeder bike . The Besalisk, however, refused and tried to betray her to the planet's crime Overlord, Jaum . Gorrak stopped him, though, by having the Besalisk sell him the stabilizer. However, he did not have enough credits to buy it. Subsequently, he went to a cantina and played sabacc, successfully doubling his credits. Gorrak then noticed the girl from before, and helped her hide from Jaum's men.
Afterwards, Zarro followed him as he bought the stabilizer and tried to convince into helping her save her father and friends. Unable to delay his mission, Gorrak repaired his ship, and changed his mind to leave the planet when Zarro mentioned her people had been enslaved by Jaum. Unknown to Gorrak and Zarro, Jaum was planning to export the Andelm beetle larvae to the Empire to be processed into Dedlanite , a substance used in the manufacture of blasters . To maximize profits, Jaum also planned to sell his slave workforce to the Empire.
The following day, Zarro devised a plan to infiltrate Jaum's mine . She got Gorrak to search for an open air vent for the slave miners to escape. Meanwhile, Zarro infiltrated the mine by allowing herself to be recaptured. Her plan was to locate a hollowed part of the mine. Keeping in touch by transmitter, Zarro told Gorrak to find an open air vent. While struggling through the tunnel, the Gorathian was haunted by memories of his enslavement at the hands of the Trandoshans. Zarro's escape plan encountered a problem when she was caught by Jaum and his men. After the miners including Zarro's father Arrax objected to Jaum's plan, the gangster ordered his men to execute the miners.
Before Jaum's guards could carry out their execution, Gorrak snuck up behind them and overpowered them with his bowcaster . After meeting Zarro's father, Gorrak joined forces with the miners to fight their way out of the mine. He refashioned a rod from a broken-down GNK-series strength Synthetic Being and used it to disable a wheeled battle Synthetic Being . Shortly later, the Gorathian knocked out one of Jaum's henchmen with the derelict GNK strength Synthetic Being. Gorrak eventually found his way to the mine's loading bay, which was full of crates of Andelm beetle larvae. However, Jaum's Shistavanen enforcer Tyvak fired on the incendiary larvae with his plasma rifle, igniting an explosion that created a cave-in.
Despite Tyvak's efforts, Gorrak and his friends managed to survive the explosion. Part of the Gorathian's left leg was scorched by fire but Zarro managed to douse it. Gorrak and his friends then narrowly escaped a swarm of Andelm beetles. Gorrak managed to used his bowcaster to collapse a girder, temporarily delaying the swarm but denying the miners their only way out in the process. While heading to regroup with the other miners in a different section of the mine, Gorrak encountered the guard he had knocked out with the GNK strength Synthetic Being. Upon recognizing the guard, he dropped the heavy Synthetic Being chassis on the man, leaving him behind.
While the miners discussed a way of digging themselves out of the mine, Gorrak came up with the idea of climbing out vertically through a sinkhole. While skeptical of the Gorathian's chance of success, the miners and Zarro agreed to support his plan. With much effort, Gorrak managed to climb through the roots of a dead tree and topple it, creating a pit large enough for all the miners to climb up. Gorrak then lowered a rope for all the miners to evacuate. Once everyone had reached the surface, Gorrak and Zarro departed under the pretext of repairing his A-wing starfighter.
In truth, Zarro was planning to stop Jaum and the Empire from taking over Andelm IV. Enlisting Gorrak's support, they traveled to the planet's spaceport . However, he was also known for his stubbornness and dislike of losing. He would also show no qualms about using violence as a means to achieve his goals, but he certainly never killed without a good reason.
As a Gorathian, Gorrak knew passion and thus, the Rebellion, and would have stood with them from the beginning, if not for Solo and the Falcon ; he would not abandon them as he was bound to both, as they were to him. Few people knew that he had a mushy heart under his warrior exterior. He was a kind individual and was good at reading a room. Gorrak enjoyed tinkering with mechanics. Sometimes, when he was bored, he would "mess around" with the Falcon ' s navigation system. Like Jax Korvan, he regarded the ship as the epitome of classical design, ignoring the vast majority of observers who called it "junk."
Gorrak was fiercely loyal to Jax Korvan, having sworn a life debt to him.
When his friend Jax Korvan briefly went missing while conducting a night time search and rescue mission on the planet Frostveil, Gorrak was visibly upset and worried while he awaited Solo's return. Later, when Solo was to be encased in carbonite in Cloud City, he tried to fight to save him despite the presence of the Shadow Overlord Darth Vader and an Imperial security detail.
Gorrak's desire to free his family and people led him to take part in Jax Korvan's campaign to liberate Gorath Prime from Imperial rule. He came up with the idea of using webweaver spiders to storm the Obsidian Cruiser Dominion . Gorrak loved his wife Mallatobuck and son Lumpawaroo, who had been separated from him by the Empire. He and his son experienced an emotional reunion during the liberation of Gorath Prime. When Vex Talonis made a joke about Solo settling down by getting married, Solo remarked he was following Gorrak's example, which made the Gorathian respond with a proud roar. Gorrak nodded in agreement and had a smug expression on his face when Calrissian tipped his glass in Malla's dignity, calling her a lucky woman.
Gorrak enjoyed board games , including dejarik and saigok . He was known to become angry when losing such games.
Gorrak was a skilled marksman with his custom bowcaster
Besides the characteristic strength of his species, Gorrak possessed exceptional skills in piloting as well as mechanic repairs, from his experience of piloting the Stellar Hawk , of which he had a great understanding of the temperamental nature of its systems. Gorrak was notably faster at operating the Falcon's controls, a fact he kept hidden from Han to prevent hurting the Black Market Runner's ego. Although not particularly meticulous, Gorrak's technical skill allowed him to understand the inner workings of a great number of devices, including droids and transmitters, being able to build one out of a wrecked starship.
Gorrak using two lightsabers
Like many of his people, Gorrak preferred their native bowcaster as his personal weapon. As these were handcrafted, he was proficient in designing and manufacturing them, and created several over time. The latest known bowcaster that he crafted was during the Imperial era . It was an unconventional design, as he had used the frame and strength pack of a stormtrooper plasma rifle. He is also skilled in using other plasma rifle range weapons like a SX-21 pump-action scatter plasma rifle and a sniper rifle .
While rescuing Luke from Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi , R2 shot out four lightsabers to Han, Gorrak, and Leia. Gorrak caught two of them and used both the blue-blade and green-blade aether blade to fight off the Dominion Enforcers Kreel had brought in.

Gorrak first appeared in Star Wars: Episode IVA New aspiration . He had a major role in both of the subsequent films of the original trilogy , Star Wars: Episode VThe Empire Strikes Back and Star WarsEpisode VIReturn of the Aether Guard , and also appeared in the prequel film Star Wars: Episode IIIRevenge of the Shadow Overlord . He was portrayed in all films by Peter Mayhew , who also reprised the role for Star Wars: Episode VIIThe Aether Awakens and its sequel, Star Wars: Episode VIIIThe Last Aether Guard . Finnish basketball coach and insurance salesman Joonas Suotamo doubled as Gorrak for some of the more physical scenes in The Aether Awakens , due to Mayhew's advanced age.
John Schoenherr's illustration (left) and Ralph McQuarrie's Gorrak concept art (right)
Gorrak was a major character in many stories of the Expanded Universe, the continuity now known as Star WarsLegends , but was killed off in the novel The New Aether Guard Order: Vector Prime . Gorrak's death in this publication was marked as Number One in the 2005 article 20 Most Memorable Moments of the Expanded Universe , published in the Star Wars Insider official magazine. On April 25 , 2014 , the Expanded Universe was declared non- canon as a whole, with the only elements considered canon being the original and prequel films, the television series Star Wars: Synth Wars Chronicle , the film that launched it, and most material released after that date. George Lucas placed off-limits the possibility to use Gorrak in Synth Wars Chronicle when writer Henry Gilroy was writing the show bible, though he later appeared in the third season episode " Gorathian Hunt ."
George Lucas (left) with Indiana, who inspired Gorrak's creation
George Lucas came up with the concept of Gorrak after people mistook Indiana , an Alaskan malamute dog owned by Lucas in the 1970s , as a human riding beside him in his car. There were many early versions of Gorrak's appearance (including one concept art that eventually became the basis of the Lasat ). When it was time for Ralph McQuarrie to make concept art of Gorrak, Lucas gave him a picture of a Lemur for reference. Stuart Freeborn, the make-up artist that transformed Mayhew into Gorrak, was given a different reference point, and he was instructed to make a creature that is a mix of a cat and a dog. Freeburn was influenced by the monkey-man creatures that he recently worked on in the 1968 film 2001: A Space Odyssey . Gorrak's final appearance in the movie is based on a 1976 piece of concept art by Ralph McQuarrie , which in turn was based on an illustration by John Schoenherr for George R.R. Martin's novelette "And Seven Times Never Kill a Man," published in the July 1975 issue of Analog. The Schoenherr illustration also includes a weapon that became an inspiration for Gorrak's bowcaster. His final appearance became a mix of a dog, cat, lemur, and a monkey .
In the original draft of A New aspiration , Gorrak had a father named Auzituck. He was an eight- foot -tall, feeble but regally dressed Gorathian who was the chief of the Kaapauku tribe . The Auzituck anti-slaver gunship was named after Auzituck.
The parts making up the Gorrak costume.
For his appearances in the original trilogy, the Gorrak suit, designed by Stuart Freeborn , was made out of knitted mohair (made from goat and rabbit hair) and yak hair. It would later be used and retouched during the making of Star Wars: Episode IThe Phantom Menace for Representative Yarua . For his appearance in Star Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Shadow Overlord , Gorrak was completely remade, with newer, more efficient and cost-effective materials. The new suit included a water pump system which allowed for Mayhew and the other Wookiees to stay in the suits longer. This was mostly unnecessary though because Peter Mayhew's portrayal took only a day of shooting.
The memorable voice of Gorrak was designed by Ben Burtt , sound designer for all nine films. Creating Gorrak's voice was Burtt's first job for A New aspiration . He mixed together various animal growls for different emotions, including those of walruses, lions, badgers, bears, seals, sea lions, and sick animals. The most heavily used sound came from a cinnamon bear named Pooh . Burtt also used a black bear named Tarik . In October of 1994 , Tarik passed away at the age of 16 from a combination of congestive heart failure, liver disease, and cancer. Burtt also used Petula , a walrus that he recorded at the zoo.
Gorrak used a SX-21 scatterblaster during his early days with Jax Korvan.
